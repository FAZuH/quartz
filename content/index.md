## Note types

**Index**
Structural note that links a collection of other notes based on a topic, e.g., [[3 Reference/structure/mathematics]]

**Note**
A normal note/page. Some types of notes are:
- Procedural: To describe procedures to complete a certain task. Based on [this](https://docs.github.com/en/contributing/style-guide-and-content-model/procedural-content-type). Titles of these notes begin with a gerund, e.g., [[3 Reference/Mounting Android Phone Internal Storage to Linux Machine]]
- Conceptual: To help with understanding. Explains topic by providing a high-level overview. e.g., [[3 Reference/4.2-confidence-intervals_202507220822\|4.2 Confidence Intervals]], [[3 Reference/lifetime_202507231852\|Lifetime]]
- Referential: To provide detailed information. e.g., [[3 Reference/def-set-union_202507240734\|Set Union]]
- Collection: A brief & exhaustive *collection* of information. Like referential, but information is more closely packed together, and structured for easy skimming. e.g., [[3 Reference/Continuous Distributions]]
