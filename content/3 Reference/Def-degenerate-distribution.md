A probability distribution is called a **degenerate distribution** if it is 1 at a point, 0 elsewhere.

A distribution is also degenerate if:
1. $$
f(x)=\begin{cases}
1 & x=\theta \\
0, & \text{others}
\end{cases}
$$
2. $$
F(x) = \begin{cases}
0, & x<\theta \\
1, & x\geq \theta
\end{cases}
$$
3. $M_{X}(t)=e^{ct}$
4. $Var(X)=0$
