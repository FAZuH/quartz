## Theorem

Let _$a,b \in \mathbb R$_

1. $(a\neq 0 \wedge a \cdot b = 1) \Rightarrow \textcolor{red}{b = 1/a}$
2. $a \cdot b = 0 \Rightarrow \textcolor{red}{(a = 0 \vee b = 0)}$ ^c
