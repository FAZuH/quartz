## Definition

Let $A,B$: Two [[3 Reference/def-set_202510022014\|sets]]

If $A\cap B = \emptyset$

Then we say $A$ and $B$ are **disjoint**

Let $(\mathbf{A}_{n}):=(A_{1},A_{2},\dots,A_{n})$ : Sequence of sets

If $$ A_{i}\cap A_{j}=\emptyset, \quad \forall i,j\in \mathbb{N}, i\neq j $$

Then we say $\mathbf{A}_{n}$ is **mutually disjoint**
