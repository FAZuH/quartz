## Definition

Let $A,B$: Two [[def-set_202510022014|sets]]

If $A\cap B = \emptyset$

Then we say $A$ and $B$ are **disjoint**

Let $(\mathbf{A}_{n}):=(A_{1},A\_{2},\dots,A\_{n})$ : Sequence of sets

If $$ A\_{i}\cap A\_{j}=\emptyset, \quad \forall i,j\in \mathbb{N}, i\neq j $$

Then we say $\mathbf{A}\_{n}$ is **mutually disjoint**
