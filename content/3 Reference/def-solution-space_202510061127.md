## Definition


The [[3 Reference/Def-solution-of-linear-system\|solution]] [[3 Reference/def-set_202510022014\|set]] of a [[3 Reference/Def-homogeneous-linear-system\|homogeneous system]] in $n$ unknowns is a [[3 Reference/def-subspace_202509240725\|subspace]], which we often refer to as the **solution space** of the system.
