## Definition

Let:
- $P$ : A proposition or action
- $\text{Natural}(P)$ : $P$ is natural

Then an argument commits the **Appeal to Nature** fallacy if and only if it asserts:
$$
\text{Natural}(P) \implies P \text{ is good/correct}
$$
