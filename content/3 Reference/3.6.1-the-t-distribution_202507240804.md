## Proof
Let
- $W\sim N(0,1)$
- $V\sim\chi^2(r)$
- $W$ and $V$ independent

Then [[3 Reference/def-random-vector_202507171028#Joint probability density function (pdf)\|joint pdf]] of $W$ and $V$ is the product of the pdf of $W$ and that of $V$:
$$
h(w,v) = \begin{cases}
 \frac{1}{2\pi}e^{-w^2/2} \frac{1}{\Gamma(r/2)2^{r/2}}v^{r/2-1}e^{-v/2} & -\infty<w<\infty,\quad 0<v<\infty \\
0 & \text{elsewhere}
\end{cases}
$$
