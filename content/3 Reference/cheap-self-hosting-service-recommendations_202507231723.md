- Why not self-hosted: Steep learning curve. Not free

**Providers**

- https://contabo.com: Singapore Ubuntu VPS with 150GB SSD, 8GB RAM, 3vCPU
- https://cloudflare.com: Domain registrar (.com):

**Costs** (as of 2025-07-23)
