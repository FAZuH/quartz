## Theorem

Let $A$ be an [[def-inverse-matrix_202509241225|invertible]] $n\times n$ [[Def-matrix|matrix]]

Then to find the [[def-inverse-matrix_202509241225|inverse]] of $A$ :

1. Find the sequence of [[theorem-elementary-row-operation_202510021405|elementary row operation]] that reduces $A$ to the [[def-identity-matrix_202509241204|identity]]
2. Perform that same sequence of operations on $I\_{n}$ to obtain $A^{-1}$
