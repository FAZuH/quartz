## Theorem

Let $A$ be an [[3 Reference/def-inverse-matrix_202509241225\|invertible]] $n\times n$ [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|matrix]]

Then to find the [[3 Reference/def-inverse-matrix_202509241225\|inverse]] of $A$ :
1. Find the sequence of [[3 Reference/theorem-elementary-row-operation_202510021405\|elementary row operation]] that reduces $A$ to the [[3 Reference/def-identity-matrix_202509241204\|identity]]
2. Perform that same sequence of operations on $I_{n}$ to obtain $A^{-1}$
