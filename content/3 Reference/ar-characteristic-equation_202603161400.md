## Definition

By expressing an AR(p) process using the [[3 Reference/backshift-operator_202603161400\|backshift operator]]:
$$(1 - \phi_1 B - \phi_2 B^2 - \dots - \phi_p B^p) Z_t = a_t$$
The characteristic equation is:
$$1 - \phi_1 x - \phi_2 x^2 - \dots - \phi_p x^p = 0$$
The process is stationary if all roots of this equation lie outside the unit circle ($|x| > 1$).
