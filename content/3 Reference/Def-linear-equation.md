## Definition
Let
- $x_{1},\dots,x_{n}$ : Variables of size $n$
- $b$ : Constant
- $a_{1},\dots,a_{n}$ : Nonzero constants

**Linear equation** is defined as $$ a_{1}x_{1} + \dots + a_{n}x_{n} = b $$

## Remark

We often say the $a_{1},\dots,a_{n}$ constants as **coefficients** instead. ^def-coefficients

A linear equation does not involve any products or roots of the $x_{i}$ variables, e.g.,  $x+3y=7$, $\frac{1}{2}x-y+3z=-1$, and not $x+3y^2=4$, $2x+2y-xy=5$.
