## Definition

If $A$ is any $m\times n$ [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|matrix]], then the transpose of $A$, denoted by $A^{T}$, is defined to be the $n\times m$ matrix that results by interchanging the rows and columns of $A$.

That is, the first column of $A^{T}$ is the first row of $A$, the second column of $A^{T}$ is the second row of $A$, and so forth.
