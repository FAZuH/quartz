## Proposition: Example 2.4.1 (a)


> Let $S\neq \emptyset, S\subseteq \mathbb R$ that is bounded above. $\forall a \in \mathbb R$:
> **$$
> \sup(a + S) = a + \sup S
> $$**

## Proposition: Example 2.4.1 (b)

> Let $A, B \subseteq \mathbb R, A, B\neq \emptyset$. $a < b\ \forall a \in A, b \in B$, then
> **$$
> \sup A \leq \inf B
> $$**

## Definition: Bounded function

Misalkan diberikan suatu fungsi $f: D \rightarrow \mathbb R$ dan range $f$, $f(D) = \set{F(x): x\in D}$

- Fungsi $f$ dikatakan **terbatas di atas** jika $f(D)$ terbatas di atas, yakni
    
    $$
    \exists B\in \mathbb R \ni f(x) \leq B\quad\forall x\in D
    $$
    
- Fungsi $f$ dikatakan **terbatas di bawah** jika $f(D)$ terbatas di bawah, yakni
    
    $$
    \exists L\in\mathbb R\ni B \leq f(x)\quad \forall x\in D
    $$
    
- Fungsi $f$ dikatakan **terbatas** jika $f$ terbatas di atas dan di bawah, yang ekivalen dengan
    
    $$
    \exists M\in\mathbb R\ni|f(x)|\leq M\quad\forall x\in D
    $$
    

## Proposition: Example 2.4.2 (a)

Misalkan $f,g$ fungsi bernilai real dengan domain $D\subseteq \mathbb R$. Misalkan pula $f,g$ terbatas. Jika $f(x) \leq g(x)$ untuk setiap $x \in D$ maka,

$$
\sup_{x \in D} f(x) \leq \sup_{x \in D} g(x).
$$

## Proposition: Example 2.4.2 (c)

Misalkan $f,g$ fungsi bernilai real dengan domain $D\subseteq \mathbb R$. Misalkan pula $f,g$ terbatas. Jika $f(x)\leq g(y)$ untuk setiap $x,y\in D$ maka,

$$
\sup_{x \in D} f(x) \leq \sup_{x \in D} g(x).
$$

## 2.4.3 Theorem: Archimedean property

> If $x\in \mathbb R$, then there exists $n_x \in \mathbb N$ such that $x \leq n_x$.

Other forms:
- $x \in \mathbb R  \rightarrow \exists n_x\in \mathbb N \ni x\leq n_x$.

- There is **always a bigger natural number than any arbitrary real number**.

> [!TIP] For proofing, use:
> - Proof by contradiction
> - [[3 Reference/2.3 The Completeness Property of R#2.3.6 Completeness property of R\|Completeness Property]]
> - $\mathbb N$

## 2.4.4 Corollary: From 2.4.3

> If $S := \set{\frac 1 n : n \in \mathbb N}$, then $\inf S = 0$.

$S := \set{\frac 1 n : n \in \mathbb N} \to \inf S = 0.$

## 2.4.5 Corollary

> If $t > 0$, then there exists $n_t \in \mathbb N$ such that $0 < \frac{1}{n_t} < t$.

$t > 0 \to \exists n_t \in \mathbb N \ni 0 < \frac{1}{n_t} < t.$

## 2.4.6 Corollary

> If $y > 0$, then there exists $n_y \in \mathbb N$ such that $n_y - 1 \leq y \leq n_y$.

$y > 0 \to \exists n_y \in \mathbb N \ni n_y - 1 \leq y \leq n_y.$

## 2.4.7 Theorem: Existence of irrational number

> There exists a positive real number $x$ such that $x^2 = 2$.

$\exists x \in \mathbb P \ni x^2 = 2.$

## 2.4.8 Theorem: Density of rational numbers at R

> If $x,y \in \mathbb R$ such that $x < y$, then there exists $r \in \mathbb Q$ such that $x < r < y$.

$x, y \in R, x < y \to \exists r \in \mathbb Q \ni x < r < y.$

## 2.4.9 Corollary: From 2.4.8

> If $x, y \in \mathbb R$ such that $x < y$, then there exists $t \in \mathbb Q^c$ such that $x < t < y$.

$x, y \in R, x < y \to \exists t \in \mathbb Q^c \ni x < t < y.$
