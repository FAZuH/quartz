> [!NOTE]
> Consider the conditional statement $p \to q$. 
> Where $p$ is a **hypothesis**, $q$ is a **conclusion**.
> 
> - **Negation**: $\neg p$
> - **Converse**: $q \to p$
> - **Inverse**: $\neg p \to \neg q$
> - **Biconditional**: $\neg p \iff \neg q$
> - **Contrapositive**: $\neg q \to \neg p$
> 
> An important property of contrapositive is that:
> $$(p \to q) \iff (\neg q \to \neg p)$$

## Various notation in algebra

> Let *$a, b \in \mathbb R,\quad n \in \mathbb N,\quad m,n \in \mathbb Z$*
> $$
> \begin{align*} ab &:= a\cdot b\\ a-b &:= a + (-b)\\ \frac a b &:= a \cdot \frac 1 b\\ a^n &:= a\cdot a\cdot a\cdot \dots \cdot a\\ a^{-n} &:= \left(\frac 1 a \right)^n\\ a^{m+n} &:= a^m a^n \end{align*}
> $$
