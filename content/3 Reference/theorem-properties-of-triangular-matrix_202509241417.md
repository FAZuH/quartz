## Theorem

- (a) The transpose of a lower [[3 Reference/def-triangular-matrix_202509241413\|triangular matrix]] is upper triangular, and the transpose of an upper triangular [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|matrix]] is lower triangular.
- (b) The product of lower triangular matrices is lower triangular, and the product of upper triangular matrices is upper triangular.

**Inverse**
- (c) A triangular matrix is [[3 Reference/def-inverse-matrix_202509241225\|invertible]] if and only if its diagonal entries are all nonzero.
- (d) The inverse of an invertible lower triangular matrix is lower triangular, and the inverse of an invertible upper triangular matrix is upper triangular.
