## Definition

**Rational numbers** $(\mathbb Q)$: Elements in $\mathbb Q$ are those that can be written in the form of

$$
Q := \left\{
	\frac p q :
	p, q \in \mathbb Z,
	q \neq 0
\right\}
$$
