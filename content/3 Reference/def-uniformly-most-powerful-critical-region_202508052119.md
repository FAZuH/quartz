## Definition

Let $C$ : [[def-test_202508050827|Critical region]] of [[def-size-of-critical-region_202507291323|size]] $\alpha$ for testing the [[def-types-of-statistical-hypotheses_202508051003|simple hypothesis]] $H\_{0}$ against an  [[def-types-of-statistical-hypotheses_202508051003|alternative composite hypothesis]] $H\_{1}$

If $C$ is a [[def-best-critical-region_202508052110|best critical region]] of size $\alpha$ for testing $H\_{0}$ against each simple hypothesis in $H\_{1}$

Then we say $C$ is a **uniformly most powerful (UMP) critical region** of size $\alpha$
