## Definition

**Gamma Distribution** for survival: Another generalization of exponential.

$$f(t) = \frac{\lambda^\alpha t^{\alpha-1} e^{-\lambda t}}{\Gamma(\alpha)}, \quad t > 0$$

$$E(T) = \frac{\alpha}{\lambda}, \quad V(T) = \frac{\alpha}{\lambda^2}$$
