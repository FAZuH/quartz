## Definition

Let $T$ be [[3 Reference/def-survival-time_202603281500\|Survival Time]]

**Median life** (denoted $t_{0.5}$) is the time where exactly 50% of subjects have experienced the event and 50% have not.

> [!TIP] Interpretation
> The **median survival time** — half the population has failed by this time, half remains.

## Property

The median life satisfies:

$$S(t_{0.5}) = 0.5$$

Or equivalently:

$$F(t_{0.5}) = 0.5$$
