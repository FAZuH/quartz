Except for 3, all of these methods **require that we already know the value of the limit**, and we then verify that our suspicion is correct.

We can use the following definitions/theorems the conditions matches:

1. [3.1.3](3.1%20Sequences%20and%20Their%20Limits.md#3.1.3%20Definition%20Converging%20and%20diverging%20(limit)%20of%20a%20sequence), [3.1.5](3.1%20Sequences%20and%20Their%20Limits.md#3.1.5%20Theorem%20Sequence%20limit%20equivalency). (*Often difficult*)
2. [3.1.10](3.1%20Sequences%20and%20Their%20Limits.md#3.1.10%20Theorem%20Finding%20limit%20of%20a%20sequence): Obtain by a multiple of the terms in a sequence converging to $0$
3. [3.1.9](3.1%20Sequences%20and%20Their%20Limits.md#3.1.9%20Theorem%20Limit%20of%20a%20tail), [3.2.3](3.2%20Limit%20Theorems.md#3.2.3%20Theorem%20Convergence%20and%20algebra%20operations), [3.2.9](3.2%20Limit%20Theorems.md#3.2.9%20Theorem%20Limit%20of%20absolute%20sequences), [3.2.10](3.2%20Limit%20Theorems.md#3.2.10%20Theorem%20Limit%20of%20square%20root%20of%20sequences): Obtain from other converging sequences.
4. [3.2.7](3.2%20Limit%20Theorems.md#3.2.7%20Theorem%20Squeeze%20theorem): Squeeze between two sequences that has the same limit.
5. [3.2.11](3.2%20Limit%20Theorems.md#3.2.11%20Theorem%20Ratio%20test%20for%20convergence%20to%20zero): Ratio test.
6. [3.3.2](3.3%20Monotone%20Sequences.md#3.3.2%20Theorem%20Monotone%20convergence): Sequence is monotone.
7. [3.4.9](3.4%20Subsequences%20and%20the%20Bolzano-Weierstrass%20Theorem.md#3.4.9%20Theorem): All convergent subsequence converges to the same limit.
8. [3.4.2](3.4%20Subsequences%20and%20the%20Bolzano-Weierstrass%20Theorem.md#3.4.2%20Theorem%20Convergence%20of%20a%20subsequence): Subsequence from a sequence converges to the same limit.
9. [3.5.5](3.5%20The%20Cauchy%20Criterion.md#3.5.5%20Thoerem%20Cauchy%20Converge%20Criterion), [3.5.8](3.5%20The%20Cauchy%20Criterion.md#3.5.8%20Theorem): Cauchy & contractive sequence
