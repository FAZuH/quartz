- [[3 Reference/def-size-of-critical-region_202507291323\|Significance level]] of test ($\alpha$)

## Example

Let $X$ : [[3 Reference/Def-random-variable\|Random variable]] with $X\sim N(\theta,100)$

Let $n=25$, thus the [[3 Reference/Def-sample-space\|sample space]] is 
$$
\mathcal{D} = \{ (x_{1},x_{2},\dots,x_{25}):-\infty<x_{i}<\infty, i=1,2,\dots 25 \}
$$

Let the [[3 Reference/def-test_202508050827\|critical region]] $C\in \mathcal{D}$ be
$$ C = \{ (x_{1},x_{2},\dots,x_{25}):x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{25}>(25)(75) \} $$

Notice that $\sum_{i=1}^{25} x_{i}>(25)(75)\iff  \bar{x}>75$. Therefore, 
$$ C = \{ (x_{1},x_{2},\dots,x_{25}):\bar{x}>75 \} $$

- We reject $H_{0} : \theta\leq75$ and accept $H_{1} : \theta>75$ if and only if the sample mean is $\bar{x}>75$
- If $\bar{x}<75$, we accept $H_{0} : \theta\leq75$

Because $X\sim(\theta,100)$, then $\bar{X}\sim(\theta,4)$ and $\frac{\bar{X}-\theta}{2}\sim N(0,1)$. We can then obtain
$$
\begin{align}
\gamma_{C}(\theta) & = \operatorname{Pr}(\bar{X}>75) \\
 & = \operatorname{Pr}\left( \frac{\bar{X}-\theta}{2} > \frac{75-\theta}{2} \right) \\
 & = 1 - \operatorname{Pr}\left( \frac{\bar{X}-\theta}{2} \leq \frac{75-\theta}{2}\right) \\
 & = 1 - \Phi\left( \frac{75-\theta}{2} \right)\quad\quad \text{(Because } \frac{\bar{X}-\theta}{2}\sim N(\theta,1))
\end{align}
$$
where $\Phi(x)$ is a distribution function from [[3 Reference/Continuous Distributions#Normal distribution\|Normal distribution]]
