## Definition: Null and alternative hypothesis
## Definition

Let $X$ : [[3 Reference/Def-random-variable\|Random variable]] with [[3 Reference/Def-probability-density-function-(pdf)\|pdf]] : $f(x;\theta), \theta\in \Omega$

Suppose due to theory/preliminary experiment
- $w_{0}, w_{1}$ disjoint subsets of $\Omega$, (i.e., $w_{0}\cup w_{1}=\Omega$)
- $\theta \in \omega_{0}$ or $\theta \in \omega_{1}$

Then 
- We label these **hypotheses** as
	$$
	\begin{align}
	H_{0} & : \theta\in \omega_{0} \\
	H_{1} & : \theta\in \omega_{1}
	\end{align}
	$$
- We refer the hypothesis $H_{0}$ as the **null hypothesis**
- We refer the hypothesis $H_{1}$ as the **alternative hypothesis**

## Definition: Test, critical region, decision rule
## Definition
Let
- $X$ : [[3 Reference/Def-random-variable\|Random variable]] wth [[3 Reference/Def-probability-density-function-(pdf)\|pdf]] $f(x;\theta),\theta\in \Omega$
- $X_{1},\dots,X_{n}$ : [[3 Reference/Def-random-sample\|Random sample]] of $X$
- $\mathcal{D}=\operatorname{space}\{(X_{1},\dots,X_{n})\}$ : [[3 Reference/mathstat1.1#Definition Sample space\|Sample space]]

Suppose
- $H_{0}$ : Null [[3 Reference/def-hypothesis_202508050806\|hypothesis]]
- $H_{1}$ : Alternative [[3 Reference/def-hypothesis_202508050806\|hypothesis]]

Then
- A **test** of $H_{0}$ versus $H_{1}$ is based on a [[3 Reference/def-subset_202507240733\|subset]] $C\subset \mathcal{D}$
- We call $C$ the **critical region**
- **Decision rule** (test) of $C$ is
$$
\begin{array}{rl}
\text{Reject }H_{0}\; (\text{Accept }H_{1}) & \text{if } (X_{1},\dots,X_{n}) \in C \\
\text{Retain }H_{0}\; (\text{Reject }H_{1}) & \text{if } (X_{1},\dots,X_{n}) \in C\end{array}
$$


## Definition: Test error types
## Definition

Suppose
- $H_{0}$ : Null [[3 Reference/def-hypothesis_202508050806\|hypothesis]]
- $H_{1}$ : Alternative [[3 Reference/def-hypothesis_202508050806\|hypothesis]]

Then
- We say a **Type I** error occurs if $H_{0}$ is rejected when $H_{0}$ is true
- We say a **Type II** error occurs if $H_{0}$ is accepted when $H_{1}$ is true


## Definition 4.5.1: Size of a critical region (or significane level)


## Definition

if
$ \alpha=\max_{\theta\in \omega_{0}}P_{\theta}[(X_{1},\dots,X_{n})\in C] $$
Then 
- We say [[3 Reference/def-test_202508050827\|critical region]] $C$ is of **size** $\alpha$
- We often say $\alpha$ is the **significance level** of the [[3 Reference/def-test_202508050827\|test]] associated with $C$
- The size/significance level $\alpha$ is the probability of making a [[3 Reference/def-test-error-types_202508050828\|type I error]]

## Definition: Power of a test
## Definition

Let $\theta\in \omega_{1}$[^1] 

Then
- $ 1-P_{\theta}[\text{Type II Error}] = \textcolor{yellow}{P_{\theta}[(X_{1},\dots,X_{n})\in C]} $$
- We say $P_{\theta}[(X_{1},\dots,X_{n})\in C]$ the **power** of the test at $\theta$


## Definition: Power function
## Definition

We define **[[3 Reference/def-power-of-a-test_202508050833\|power]] function** of a [[3 Reference/def-test_202508050827\|critical region]] to be 
$ \gamma_{C}(\theta)=P_{\theta}[(X_{1},\dots,X_{n})\in C];\quad\theta\in \omega_{1} $$

Suppose $C_{1},C_{2}$ : Critical regions, both of [[3 Reference/def-size-of-critical-region_202507291323\|size]] $\alpha$

Then $C_{1}$ is better than $C_{2}$ if $\gamma_{C_{1}}(\theta)\geq \gamma_{C_{2}}(\theta), \forall \theta\in \omega_{1}$



## Remark: Types of hypothesis tests
## Definition

A **statistical hypothesis** is a question a distribution from one or more [[3 Reference/Def-random-variable\|random variables]]

A **simple statistical hypothesis** specifies completely about a distribution. e.g., $H_{0}: \theta=75$ and $H_{1}: \theta=77$

A **composite statistical hypothesis** specifies in-completely about a distribution. e.g., $H_{0} : \theta\leq 75$ and $H_{1} : \theta>75$


## Remark: Equivalent terms

The following terms are <u>equivalent</u>:
- [[3 Reference/def-size-of-critical-region_202507291323\|Significance level]] of test ($\alpha$)
- [[3 Reference/def-size-of-critical-region_202507291323\|Size]] of [[3 Reference/def-test_202508050827\|critical region]] ($\alpha$)
- [[3 Reference/def-power-of-a-test_202508050833\|Power of a test]] if $H_{0}$ is true
- Probability of [[3 Reference/def-test-error-types_202508050828\|type I error]]

## Example

Let $X$ : [[3 Reference/Def-random-variable\|Random variable]] with $X\sim N(\theta,100)$

Let $n=25$, thus the [[3 Reference/Def-sample-space\|sample space]] is 
$$
\mathcal{D} = \{ (x_{1},x_{2},\dots,x_{25}):-\infty<x_{i}<\infty, i=1,2,\dots 25 \}
$$

Let the [[3 Reference/def-test_202508050827\|critical region]] $C\in \mathcal{D}$ be
$$ C = \{ (x_{1},x_{2},\dots,x_{25}):x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{25}>(25)(75) \} $$

Notice that $\sum_{i=1}^{25} x_{i}>(25)(75)\iff  \bar{x}>75$. Therefore, 
$$ C = \{ (x_{1},x_{2},\dots,x_{25}):\bar{x}>75 \} $$

- We reject $H_{0} : \theta\leq75$ and accept $H_{1} : \theta>75$ if and only if the sample mean is $\bar{x}>75$
- If $\bar{x}<75$, we accept $H_{0} : \theta\leq75$

Because $X\sim(\theta,100)$, then $\bar{X}\sim(\theta,4)$ and $\frac{\bar{X}-\theta}{2}\sim N(0,1)$. We can then obtain
$$
\begin{align}
\gamma_{C}(\theta) & = \operatorname{Pr}(\bar{X}>75) \\
 & = \operatorname{Pr}\left( \frac{\bar{X}-\theta}{2} > \frac{75-\theta}{2} \right) \\
 & = 1 - \operatorname{Pr}\left( \frac{\bar{X}-\theta}{2} \leq \frac{75-\theta}{2}\right) \\
 & = 1 - \Phi\left( \frac{75-\theta}{2} \right)\quad\quad \text{(Because } \frac{\bar{X}-\theta}{2}\sim N(\theta,1))
\end{align}
$$
where $\Phi(x)$ is a distribution function from [[3 Reference/Continuous Distributions#Normal distribution\|Normal distribution]]
