## Definition

In a $2\times2$ contingency table, the **[[def-odds_202509221037|odds]] ratio** is defined as
$$
\theta = \frac{\text{odds}\_1}{\text{odds}\_2} = \frac{\pi\_1/(1-\pi\_1)}{\pi\_2/(1-\pi\_2)}
$$
, where:

- $\text{odds}\_{1}$ : The odds of row 1
- $\text{odds}\_{2}$ : The odds of row 2
- $\pi\_{1}$ : Probability of success of variable

\end{align}
$$

## Remark

The further away $\theta$ is from 1, the more evidence there is against independency between the two variables.

If the two variables are perfectly independent, then $\pi\_{1}=\pi\_{2}$, thus $\theta=1$.
