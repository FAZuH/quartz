## Definition

In a $2\times2$ contingency table, the **[[3 Reference/def-odds_202509221037\|odds]] ratio** is defined as
 $$
\theta = \frac{\text{odds}_1}{\text{odds}_2} = \frac{\pi_1/(1-\pi_1)}{\pi_2/(1-\pi_2)}
$$
, where:
- $\text{odds}_{1}$ : The odds of row 1
- $\text{odds}_{2}$ : The odds of row 2
- $\pi_{1}$ : Probability of success of variable

## Remark

The further away $\theta$ is from 1, the more evidence there is against independency between the two variables.

If the two variables are perfectly independent, then $\pi_{1}=\pi_{2}$, thus $\theta=1$.
