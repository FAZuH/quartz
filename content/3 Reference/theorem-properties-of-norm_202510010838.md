## Theorem

Let

- $\mathbf{v}$ vector in $R^n$
- $k$ any [[Def-matrix|scalar]]

Then

- $||\mathbf{v}||\geq 0$
- $||\mathbf{v}|| = 0\iff \mathbf{v}=0$
- $||k\mathbf{v}||=|k|; ||\mathbf{v}||$
