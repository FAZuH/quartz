## Definition

Let
- $A_{1},\dots, A_{k}$ : Events
- $P(A_{i})>0, \quad i=1,\dots, k$
- $B$ : Event

Suppose $A_{1},\dots, A_{k}$ form a partition of $\mathcal C$

Then $$ P(A_{j}|B) = \frac{P(A_{j})P(B|A_{j})}{\sum_{i=1}^kP(A_{i})P(B|A_{i})} $$
