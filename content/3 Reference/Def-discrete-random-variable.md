## Definition

Let
- $X$ : [[3 Reference/mathstat1.5#Definition 1.5.1 Random variable\|Random variable]]
- $\mathcal{D}$ : Space of $X$

If $\mathcal{D}$ is either finite or countable

Then we say $X$ is a **discrete random variable**
