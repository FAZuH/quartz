## Definition

A **Yule Process** is a [[3 Reference/pure-birth-process_202605080516\|pure birth process]] with linear birth rate:

$$\lambda_n = n\lambda, \quad n \geq 0$$

where each member of the population independently gives birth at exponential rate $\lambda$.

The total birth rate when there are $n$ individuals is $n\lambda$, since each of the $n$ individuals acts independently.

> [!TIP] Interpretation
> Named after G. Yule, who used it in his mathematical theory of evolution. Each existing member has the same chance of producing a new member per unit time. The more members there are, the faster the population grows — exponential growth in expectation.

## Properties

- Transition rates: $q_{n,n+1} = n\lambda$, $q_{n,n} = -n\lambda$
- Transition probabilities: $P_{n,n+1} = 1$
- The sojourn time in state $n$ is $\text{Exp}(n\lambda)$ with mean $\mathbb{E}[T_n] = 1/(n\lambda)$
