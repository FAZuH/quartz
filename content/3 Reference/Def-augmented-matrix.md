## Definition

Let [[Def-linear-system|linear system]] of $m$ equations in $n$ unknowns:
$$ \begin{matrix}
a\_{11}x\_{1} & + & a\_{12}x\_{2} & + & \dots & + &  a\_{1n}x\_{n} & = & b\_{1} \\
a\_{21}x\_{1} & + & a\_{22}x\_{2} & + & \dots & + &  a\_{2n}x\_{n} & = & b\_{2} \\
\vdots & & \vdots & &&  & \vdots &  & \vdots \\
a\_{m1}x\_{1} & + & a\_{m2}x\_{2} & + & \dots & + &  a\_{mn}x\_{n} & = & b\_{m} \\
\end{matrix} $$

Then the **augmented [[Def-matrix|matrix]]** of the linear system is written as
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
a\_{11} & a\_{12} & \dots & a\_{1n} & b\_{1} \\
a\_{21} & a\_{22} & \dots & a\_{2n} & b\_{2}  \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots \\
a\_{m1} & a\_{m2} & \dots & a\_{mn} & b\_{m}
\end{bmatrix}
$$
