## Theorem
> Let:
> - $X$ random variable
> - $u(X) \to \mathbb{P}$
> - $E[u(X)]$ exists $\forall c\in \mathbb{P}$
> 
> Then
> $$
> P[u(X)\geq c] \leq \frac{E[u(X)]}{c}
> $$
