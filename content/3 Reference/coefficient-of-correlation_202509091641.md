## Interpretation

The **Pearson product moment coefficient correlation** (or correlation) between two variables, say $x$ and $y$, is a quantity to measure the strength of the linear relationship between the variables

## Formula

$$
\begin{align*}
\rho = corr(x,y) &= \frac{E(X-\mu_X)(E(Y-\mu_Y)}{\sigma_X\sigma_Y}\\
&= \frac{SS_{xy}}{\sqrt{SS_{xx}}\sqrt{SS_{yy}}}
\end{align*}
$$
