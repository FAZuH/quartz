## Definition 8.2.1

## Definition

Let $C$ : [[3 Reference/def-test_202508050827\|Critical region]] of [[3 Reference/def-size-of-critical-region_202507291323\|size]] $\alpha$ for testing the [[3 Reference/def-types-of-statistical-hypotheses_202508051003\|simple hypothesis]] $H_{0}$ against an  [[3 Reference/def-types-of-statistical-hypotheses_202508051003\|alternative composite hypothesis]] $H_{1}$

If $C$ is a [[3 Reference/def-best-critical-region_202508052110\|best critical region]] of size $\alpha$ for testing $H_{0}$ against each simple hypothesis in $H_{1}$

Then we say $C$ is a **uniformly most powerful (UMP) critical region** of size $\alpha$


## Definition

Let $C$ : [[3 Reference/def-uniformly-most-powerful-critical-region_202508052119\|UMP critical region]] of [[3 Reference/def-size-of-critical-region_202507291323\|size]] $\alpha$ for testing the [[3 Reference/def-types-of-statistical-hypotheses_202508051003\|simple hypothesis]] $H_{0}$ against an  alternative [[3 Reference/def-types-of-statistical-hypotheses_202508051003\|composite hypothesis]] $H_{1}$

A [[3 Reference/def-test_202508050827\|test]] defined by $C$ is called a **uniformly most powerful (UMP) test**, with [[3 Reference/def-size-of-critical-region_202507291323\|significance level]] $\alpha$, for testing the simple hypothesis $H_{0}$ against the alternative composite hypothesis $H_{1}$.



## Definition 8.2.2: Monotone likelihood ratio (mlr)
## Definition

Let $L(\theta,\mathbf{x})$ : [[3 Reference/Def-likelihood-function\|Likelihood function]]

If 
$ \frac{L(\theta_{1},\mathbf{x})}{L(\theta_{2},\mathbf{x})},\quad \forall \theta_{1}<\theta_{2} $$
is a monotone function of $y=u(\mathbf{x})$

Then we say $L(\theta,\mathbf{x})$ has **monotone likelihood ratio** (mlr) in the statistic $y=u(\mathbf{x})$
