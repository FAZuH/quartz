## Exercise
### Matrix multiplication

Consider the matrices 
$$
A=\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 4 \\ 2 & 6 & 0 \end{bmatrix},\quad
B=\begin{bmatrix} 4 & 1 & 4 & 3 \\ 0 & -1 & 3 & 1 \\ 2 & 7 & 5 & 2 \end{bmatrix}
$$

Since $A$ is a $2\times 3$ matrix, and $B$ is a $3\times 4$ matrix, the product $AB$ is a $2\times 4$ matrix.

The process of matrix multiplication is illlustrated below:

Entry of $(AB)_{23}$ is:
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 & 4 \\
\colorbox{blue}2 & \colorbox{blue}6 & \colorbox{blue}0
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
4 & 1 & \colorbox{blue}4 & 3 \\
0 & -1 & \colorbox{blue}3 & 1 \\
2 & 7 & \colorbox{blue}5 & 2
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
\square & \square & \square & \square \\
\square & \square & 26 & \square
\end{bmatrix}
$$


Entry of $(AB)_{14}$ is:
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
\colorbox{blue}1 & \colorbox{blue}2 & \colorbox{blue}4 \\
2 & 6 & 0
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
4 & 1 & 4 & \colorbox{blue}3 \\
0 & -1 & 3 & \colorbox{blue}1 \\
2 & 7 & 5 & \colorbox{blue}4
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
\square & \square & \square & 13 \\
\square & \square & \square & \square
\end{bmatrix}
$$

The computation for the remaining entries are:
$$
\begin{align}
(1\cdot 4) +(2\cdot 0)  +(4\cdot 2) & =12 \\
(1\cdot 1) -(2\cdot 1)  +(4\cdot 7) & =27 \\
(1\cdot 4) +(2\cdot 3)  +(4\cdot 5) & =30 \\
(2\cdot 4) +(6\cdot 0)  +(0\cdot 2) & =8 \\
(2\cdot 1) -(6\cdot 1)  +(0\cdot 8) & =-4 \\
(2\cdot 3) +(6\cdot 1)  +(0\cdot 2) & =12
\end{align}
$$

Obtaining
$$
AB=\begin{bmatrix}
12 & 27 & 30 & 13 \\
8 & -4 & 26 & 12
\end{bmatrix}
$$
