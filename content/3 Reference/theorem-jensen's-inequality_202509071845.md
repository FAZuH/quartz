## Theorem

> Let
> - $\phi$ is convex on an open interval $I$
> - $X$ : Random variable
> - $\mathcal{S}_{X}$ : Support of $X$
> 
> If
> - $\mathcal{S}_{X} \subseteq I$
> - $E(X)$ is finite
> 
> Then
> $$ \phi[E(X)]\leq E[\phi(X)] $$
