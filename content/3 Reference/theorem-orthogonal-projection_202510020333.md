## Theorem

Let
- $\mathbf{u},\mathbf{a}$ : Vectors in $R^n$
- $\mathbf{a}\neq \mathbf{0}$ (is not the zero vector)

If
- $\mathbf{w}_{1}$ : Vector that is scalar multiple of $\mathbf{a}$
- $\mathbf{w}_{2}$ : Vector that is [[3 Reference/def-orthogonal_202510011638\|orthogonal]] to $\mathbf{a}$

Then
- $\mathbf{u}$ can be expressed in <u>exactly one way</u> in the form of $$
\mathbf{u}=\mathbf{w}_{1}+\mathbf{w}_{2}
$$
