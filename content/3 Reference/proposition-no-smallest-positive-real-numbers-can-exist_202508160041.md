## Proposition

Notice that if $a \in \mathbb P$, and since $\frac 1 2 > 0$, we have
$$
0 < \frac 1 2 a < a
$$

Thus if it is claimed that $a$ is the smallest real positive number, we can obtain a smaller positive number $\frac 1 2 a$.
