## Definition

Let

- $X$ : [[Def-discrete-random-variable|Discrete random variable]]
- $\mathcal{D}$ : Space of $X$

If $$ p\_{X}(x) = P\[X=x], \quad \forall x\in \mathcal{D} $$

Then we say that $p\_{X}(x)$ is the **probability mass function** (pmf) of $X$

## Properties of pmfs

Note that pmfs satisfies the following properties:

1. $0\leq p\_{X}(x)\leq{1},\quad \forall x\in \mathcal{D}$
2. $\sum\_{x\in \mathcal{D}}p\_{X}(x)=1$
