## Theorem
**Gauss-Jordan elimination** is the procedure of using elementary row operation to transform an [[3 Reference/Def-augmented-matrix\|augmented matrix]] to [[3 Reference/Def-reduced-row-echelon-form-(rref)\|reduced row echelon form]].

This procedure consists of:
1. **Forward phase**: Zeros are introduced below the leading 1’s 
2. **Backward phase**: Zeros are introduced above the leading 1’s
