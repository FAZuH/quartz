## Definition: Vectors

Let

- $\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v}$ : Nonzero vectors in $R^{2}$ or $R^{3}$
- $\theta$ : Angle between $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$

Then the **dot product** of $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ (denoted by $\mathbf{u}\cdot \mathbf{v}$) is defined as
$\mathbf{u}\cdot \mathbf{v}=||\mathbf{u}||\; ||\mathbf{v}||\cos \theta$

If $\mathbf{u}=\mathbf{0}$ or $\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{0}$ then we define $\mathbf{u}\cdot \mathbf{v}$ to be $0$

## Remark

We can obtain the angle between $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ using:

$$
\cos \theta=\frac{\mathbf{u}\cdot \mathbf{v}}{||\mathbf{u}||\; ||\mathbf{v}||}
$$
