## Theorem

 **Universal set** is the [[3 Reference/def-set_202510022014\|set]] of all elements.

It is denoted by $U$ or $\Omega$.

## Remark

Notice that it is also the complement of the [[3 Reference/def-empty-set_202508171902\|empty set]].
