A **Conditional Poisson Process** is synonymous with a [[3 Reference/mixed-poisson-process_202604092032\|Mixed Poisson Process]].

It refers to a counting process where there is a positive random variable $L$ such that, conditional on $L = \lambda$, the counting process is a [[3 Reference/poisson-process_202604031845\|Poisson process]] with rate $\lambda$.

## Properties

- It has **stationary increments**.
- It does **not** have independent increments.
