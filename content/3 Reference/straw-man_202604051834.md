## Definition

Let:

- $P$ : The original proposition
- $P'$ : A distorted or weakened version of $P$

Then an argument commits the **Straw Man** fallacy if and only if it asserts:

$$
\text{I have an argument concerning } P' \implies P \text{ is false}
$$

> [!TIP] Interpretation
> Instead of addressing the actual argument, a distorted version is attacked instead.

## Examples

> [!EXAMPLE] Example 1: The Environmental Caricature
> **Scenario:** Someone proposes modest environmental regulations. Opponents argue against "shutting down all industry and making everyone live in caves."
>
> **Why it fails:** The original proposal was for moderate regulations, not industrial shutdown. The extreme version is easier to attack but doesn't represent the actual position.

> [!EXAMPLE] Example 2: The Education Misrepresentation
> **Scenario:** A teacher suggests reducing homework. Critics respond, "So you want students to learn nothing and just play video games all day?"
>
> **Why it fails:** Reducing homework isn't the same as eliminating all learning. The distorted version ignores the actual argument about homework effectiveness and student wellbeing.

> [!EXAMPLE] Example 3: The Healthcare Exaggeration
> **Scenario:** Advocating for universal healthcare, someone is confronted with arguments against "government controlling every medical decision you make."
>
> **Why it fails:** Universal healthcare systems vary widely. Many countries have universal coverage without government micromanaging individual medical choices.

> [!EXAMPLE] Example 4: The Free Speech Defense
> **Scenario:** Someone criticizes hate speech. Opponents argue they're "trying to criminalize all unpopular opinions and destroy free speech."
>
> **Why it fails:** Criticizing speech isn't the same as banning it. The opponent has shifted from addressing the critique to defending against a more extreme position that wasn't proposed.

> [!EXAMPLE] Example 5: The Simplified Economics
> **Scenario:** Someone suggests progressive taxation. Critics argue against "taking all money from successful people and giving it to those who refuse to work."
>
> **Why it fails:** Progressive taxation is a spectrum, not a binary of complete confiscation. The straw man ignores nuance and creates an easier target to attack.
