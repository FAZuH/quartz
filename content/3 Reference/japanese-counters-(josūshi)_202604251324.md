Japanese counters (助数詞, _josūshi_) — most common ones:

|Counter|Reading|Used for|
|---|---|---|
|個|こ (ko)|Small, compact objects (apples, boxes, coins)|
|本|ほん (hon)|Long, cylindrical objects (pens, bottles, rivers, roads)|
|枚|まい (mai)|Flat, thin objects (paper, shirts, plates, slices)|
|冊|さつ (satsu)|Bound books, notebooks|
|台|だい (dai)|Machines, vehicles, large appliances|
|匹|ひき (hiki)|Small animals (cats, dogs, fish, insects)|
|頭|とう (tou)|Large animals (horses, cows, elephants)|
|羽|わ (wa)|Birds and rabbits|
|杯|はい (hai)|Cups, glasses, bowls of liquid; also squid/octopus|
|膳|ぜん (zen)|Pairs of chopsticks|
|足|そく (soku)|Pairs of footwear (shoes, socks)|
|着|ちゃく (chaku)|Outfits, sets of clothing|
|軒|けん (ken)|Houses, buildings (shops, homes)|
|階|かい (kai)|Floors/stories of a building|
|室|しつ (shitsu)|Rooms|
|番|ばん (ban)|Numbers in a sequence, turns|
|回|かい (kai)|Number of times (occurrences)|
|度|ど (do)|Degrees; also times/occurrences (more formal than 回)|
|人|にん/り (nin/ri)|People (1人=ひとり, 2人=ふたり, then にん)|
|枚|まい|Already listed — very high frequency, worth double-noting|
|切れ|きれ (kire)|Slices (bread, fish fillet)|
|粒|つぶ (tsubu)|Small round things (grains, pills, beads)|
|本|—|Already listed|
|滴|てき (teki)|Drops of liquid|
|点|てん (ten)|Points, items in a list, art pieces|
|枚|—|—|
|丁|ちょう (chō)|Blocks of tofu; also guns, tools|
|缶|かん (kan)|Cans (of food/drink)|
|瓶|びん (bin)|Bottles|
|袋|ふくろ (fukuro)|Bags/sacks|
|箱|はこ (hako)|Boxes|

**Generic fallback:** 〜つ (tsu) — native Japanese counter usable for almost anything up to 9 (ひとつ、ふたつ…ここのつ), then breaks down. Use when you don't know the specific counter.

**Sound changes to watch:** counters change pronunciation based on preceding number (e.g. 一本=いっぽん, 三本=さんぼん, 六本=ろっぽん). This is rendaku/euphonic change, not random — it follows patterns per counter.
