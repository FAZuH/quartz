Mean squared error <u>measures error in a model</u>. If a model does not have any errors, then $MSE=0$. As the error goes up, MSE goes up.

$$
MSE = \frac{\sum_{i}^n (y_{i}-\hat{y}_{i})^2}{n}
$$
