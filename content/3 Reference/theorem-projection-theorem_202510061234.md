## Theorem

If $W$ is a finite-dimensional [[def-subspace_202509240725|subspace]] of an [[def-real-inner-product-space_202510061214|inner product space]] $V$, then every vector $\mathbf{u}$ in $V can be expressed in exactly one way as
$$
\mathbf{u}=\mathbf{w}_{1}+\mathbf{w}_{2}
$$
where $\mathbf{w}_{1}\in W$ and $\mathbf{w}_{2}\in W^\perp$
