## Definition

The **Ljung-Box test** is an improved version of the[[definition-box-pierce-test_202603301400|Box-Pierce test]] for testing whether all autocorrelations up to lag $m$ are jointly zero.

### Hypotheses

$H_0:\rho_1 = \rho_2 = \dots = \rho_m = 0 \quad \text{(white noise)}$

$H_1: \text{At least one } \rho_k \neq 0$

### Test Statistic

$LB = n(n+2) \sum_{k=1}^{m} \frac{r_k^2}{n-k}$

where:

- $n$ = number of observations
- $m$ = maximum lag

### Distribution

Under $H_0$: $LB \sim \chi^2_m$

### Decision Rule

Reject $H_0$ at level $\alpha$ if:

$LB > \chi^2_{m, \alpha}$

> [!TIP] Advancement over Box-Pierce
> Ljung-Box modifies the Box-Pierce statistic by weighting each $r_k^2$ by $\frac{n+2}{n-k}$, providing better $\chi^2$ approximation for small samples.

## Comparison with Box-Pierce

| Test | Formula | Best For |
|------|---------|----------|
| Box-Pierce | $Q = T\sum_{k=1}^{m} r_k^2$ | Large $n$ |
| Ljung-Box | $LB = n(n+2)\sum_{k=1}^{m} \frac{r_k^2}{n-k}$ | Small $n$ |
