## Definition
Let
- $X$ : [[3 Reference/Def-discrete-random-variable\|Discrete random variable]]
- $\mathcal{D}$ : Space of $X$

If
$$
\mathcal{S}_X = \{ x \in \mathcal{D} : P(X = x) > 0 \}
$$

Then we say $\mathcal{S}_{X}$ is the **support** of $X$

## Remark

> [!tip] Interpretation
> Notice that $P(X=x)$ must be positive. This means you can interpret support of $X$ as:
> - The set where $P(X)$ is not $0$
> - Set of all possible outcomes that can actually occur (have positive probability)
