Let 
- $X\sim b(1,p_{1})$ : [[3 Reference/Def-random-variable\|Random variable]] ([[3 Reference/Discrete Distributions#Bernoulli distribution\|Bernoulli distribution]])
- $Y\sim b(1,p_{2})$ : Random variable
- $X,Y$ independent
- $X_{1},\dots,X_{n_{1}}$ : [[3 Reference/Def-random-sample\|Random sample]] from $X$
- $Y_{1},\dots,Y_{n_{2}}$ : [[3 Reference/Def-random-sample\|Random sample]] from $Y$
- $n=n_{1}+n_{2}$

Assume the random samples are independent of one another

$$
\hat{p_{1}}-\hat{p_{2}}\pm z_{\alpha/2}\sqrt{ \frac{\hat{p_{1}}(1-\hat{p_{1}})}{n_{1}} + \frac{\hat{p_{2}}(1-\hat{p_{2}})}{n_{2}} }
$$
