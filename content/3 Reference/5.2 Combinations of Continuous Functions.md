## 5.2.1 Theorem

Let
- $A\subseteq \mathbb{R}$
- $f,g:A\to \mathbb{R}$
- $b\in \mathbb{R}$

If
- $c\in A$
- $f,g$ continuous at $c$

Then
- $f+g, f-g, fg, bf$ continuous at $c$
- $h:A\to \mathbb{R}$ continuous at $c$, $h(x)\neq0\forall x\in A\implies \frac{f}{h}$ continuous at $c$

## 5.2.2 Theorem

Let
- $A\subseteq \mathbb{R}$
- $f,g$ continuous on $A\to \mathbb{R}$
- $b\in \mathbb{R}$

Then
- $f+g, f-g, fg, bf$ continuous on $A$
- $h:A\to \mathbb{R}$ continuous at $c$, $h(x)\neq0,\forall x\in A\implies \frac{f}{h}$ continuous on $A$

## 5.2.4 Theorem

Let
- $A\subseteq \mathbb{R}$
- $f:A\to \mathbb{R}$
- $|f|$ defined by $|f|(x):=|f(x)|,\forall x\in A$

Then
- $f$ continuous at $c\in A \implies |f|$ continuous at $c$
- $f$ continuous on $A \implies |f|$ continuous on $A$

## 5.2.5 Theorem

Let
- $A\subseteq \mathbb{R}$
- $f:A\to \mathbb{R}$
- $f(x)\geq0,\forall x\in A$
- $\sqrt{ f }$ defined by $(\sqrt{ f })(x):=\sqrt{ f(x) },\forall x\in A$

Then
- $f$ continuous at $c\in A \implies \sqrt{ f }$ continuous at $c$
- $f$ continuous on $A \implies \sqrt{ f }$ continuous on $A$

## 5.2.6 Theorem

Let
- $A,B\in \mathbb{R}$
- $f:A\to \mathbb{R}$
- $g:b\to \mathbb{R}$
- $f(A)\subseteq \mathbb{B}$

If
- $f$ continuous at $c\in A$
- $g$ continuous at $b=f(c)\in B$

Then
- $g \circ f:A\to \mathbb{R}$ continuous at $c$

## 5.2.7 Theorem

Let
- $A,B\in \mathbb{R}$
- $f:A\to \mathbb{R}$ continuous on $A$
- $g:b\to \mathbb{R}$ continuous on $B$

If
- $f(A)\subseteq B$

Then
- $g\circ f:A\to \mathbb{R}$ continuous on $A$
