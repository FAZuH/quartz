## Definition

Let $X,Y$ : [[Def-random-variable|Random variables]]

If $F_{X}(x)=F_{Y}(x),\quad\forall x\in \mathbb{R}$

Then

- We say $X$ and $Y$ are **equal in distribution**
- We write $X \stackrel D= Y$
