## Definition

Let $A$ : [[3 Reference/def-inverse-matrix_202509241225\|Invertible]]

If $A^{-1}=A^T$ or equivalently, if $AA^T=A^TA=I$

Then we say $A$ is an **[[3 Reference/def-orthogonal_202510011638\|orthogonal]]** [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|matrix]]

## Alternative definition

- Invertible $A$ is **orthogonal** if $A^{-1}=A^T$, or equivalently if $AA^T=A^TA=I$
- Invertible matrix is said to be **orthogonal**, if its inverse is equal to its transpose.

## Example

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} & \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \\
-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} & \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} & -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \\
\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} & \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$
