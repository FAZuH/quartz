## Definition

Let 
- $A_{1},A_{2},\dots,A_{r}$ : [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|Matrices]] of the same size
- $c_{1},c_{2},\dots,c_{r}$ : [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|Scalars]]

Then an expression of the form
$$
c_{1}A_{1}+c_{2}A_{2}+\dots+c_{r}A_{r}
$$
is called a **linear combination** of $A_{1},A_{2},\dots,A_{r}$ with **coefficients** $c_{1},c_{2},\dots,c_{r}$.
