## Definition

Let:
- $W$ : A whole composed of parts
- $p_i$ : A part of $W$
- $P$ : A property

Then an argument commits the **Division Fallacy** if and only if it asserts:
$$
W \text{ has property } P \implies \forall p_i \in W, \ p_i \text{ has property } P
$$

> [!NOTE]
> Properties of the whole do not necessarily apply to individual parts.
