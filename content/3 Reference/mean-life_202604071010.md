## Definition

Let $T$ be [[3 Reference/def-survival-time_202603281500\|Survival Time]]

**Mean life** (also called *expected life time* or *mean lifetime*) is the expected value of the survival time:

$$\mu = E[T]$$

> [!TIP] Interpretation
> The **average survival time** across all subjects. Also known as *usia harapan hidup* (life expectancy).

## Formula

Mean life can be computed from the survival function:

$$\mu = E[T] = \int_0^\infty S(t) \, dt$$

## Relationship with Mean Residual Life

Mean life is a special case of [[3 Reference/def-mean-residual-life_202603281500\|Mean Residual Life]] at $t=0$:

$$\mu = E[T] = E[T - 0 \mid T > 0] = \text{mrl}(0)$$

## Comparison with Median Life

| Aspect | Mean Life $\mu$ | Median Life $t_{0.5}$ |
|--------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Definition** | Average: $E[T]$ | 50th percentile: $S(t)=0.5$ |
| **Formula** | $\int_0^\infty S(t)dt$ | Solve $S(t)=0.5$ for $t$ |
| **Interpretation** | Expected lifetime | Half fail before, half after |
| **Affected by** | All values (sensitive to outliers) | Only the middle value (robust) |

## Example

**Exponential distribution** with $S(t) = e^{-\lambda t}$:
- Mean life: $\mu = \int_0^\infty e^{-\lambda t}dt = \frac{1}{\lambda}$
- Median life: $e^{-\lambda t_{0.5}} = 0.5 \Rightarrow t_{0.5} = \frac{\ln 2}{\lambda} \approx \frac{0.693}{\lambda}$

Note: For exponential, mean > median (right-skewed distribution).
