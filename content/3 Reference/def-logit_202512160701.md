## Definition

Let $\pi$ : Probability of success for an [[3 Reference/Def-events\|event]] of event $A$

Logit is the logarithm of [[3 Reference/def-odds_202509221037\|Odds]] of event $A$: 
$$
\text{Logit} = \log(\text{Odds}) = \log \left( \frac{\pi}{1-\pi} \right)
$$
