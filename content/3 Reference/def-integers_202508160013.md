## Definition

**Integers** $(\mathbb Z)$: The elements $z\in\mathbb Z$ are identified as 
- $0\in\mathbb R$
- $n-$fold of $1\in\mathbb R$ 
- $n-$ fold of $-1\in\mathbb R$
