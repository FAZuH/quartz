## Definition

$X \sim \text{Bernoulli}(p)$

Models random experiment whose outcomes are "success" or "failure"

- pmf: $p^x(1-p)^{1-x}$, $x \in {0,1}$
- mean: $p$
- var: $p(1-p)$
- mgf: $1-p+pe^t$
