## Definition

$X \sim \text{Bernoulli}(p)$

Models [[Def-random-experiment|random experiment]] whose outcomes are "success" or "failure".

- [[Def-probability-mass-function-(pmf)|pmf]]: $p^x(1-p)^{1-x}$, $x \in {0,1}$
- [[Def-mean|Mean]]: $p$
- [[Def-variance|Variance]]: $p(1-p)$
- [[Def-moment-generating-function-(mgf)|mgf]]: $1-p+pe^t$
