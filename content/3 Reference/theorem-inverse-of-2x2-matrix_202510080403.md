## Theorem

Let $A = \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}$

Then

- $A$ is [[theorem-matrix-invertibility_202509241243|invertible]] if and only if $ad-bc\neq0$
-

$$
A^{-1}=\frac{1}{ad-bc}\begin{bmatrix}
d & -b \\
-c & a
\end{bmatrix}
$$
