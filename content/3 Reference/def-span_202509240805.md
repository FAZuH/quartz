## Definition

Let 
- $V$ : [[3 Reference/def-vector-space_202509240715\|Vector space]]
- $S=\{ \mathbf{w}_{1},\mathbf{w}_{2},\dots,\mathbf{w}_{r} \}\subset V$

If $W$ [[3 Reference/def-subspace_202509240725\|subspace]] of $V$ consists of all possible linear combinations of the vectors in $S$

Then
- $W$ is called the subspace of $V$ **generated** by $S$
- We say the vectors $\mathbf{w}_{1},\mathbf{w}_{2},\dots,\mathbf{w}_{r}$ **span** $W$
- We denote:
	- $W=\operatorname{span}\{ \mathbf{w}_{1},\mathbf{w}_{2},\dots,\mathbf{w}_{r} \}$
	- $W=\operatorname{span}(S)$
