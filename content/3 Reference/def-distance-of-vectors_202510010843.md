## Definition

Let

- $\mathbf{u}=(u\_{1},u\_{2},\dots,u\_{n})$ points in $R^n$
- $\mathbf{v}=(v\_{1},v\_{2},\dots,v\_{n})$ points in $R^n$

Then the **distance** between $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ denoted by $d(\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v})$ is defined by
$$
d(\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v})=||\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{v}|| = \sqrt{ (u\_{1}-v\_{1})^{2}+(u\_{2}-v\_{2})^{2}+\dots+(u\_{n}-v\_{n})^{2} }
$$
