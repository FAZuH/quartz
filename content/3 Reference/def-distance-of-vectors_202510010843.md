## Definition

Let
- $\mathbf{u}=(u_{1},u_{2},\dots,u_{n})$ points in $R^n$
- $\mathbf{v}=(v_{1},v_{2},\dots,v_{n})$ points in $R^n$

Then the **distance** between $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ denoted by $d(\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v})$ is defined by
$$
d(\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v})=||\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{v}|| = \sqrt{ (u_{1}-v_{1})^{2}+(u_{2}-v_{2})^{2}+\dots+(u_{n}-v_{n})^{2} }
$$
