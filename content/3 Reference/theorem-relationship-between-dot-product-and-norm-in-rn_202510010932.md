## Theorem

Let $\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v}$ vectors in $R^n$

Then
$$
\mathbf{u}\cdot \mathbf{v}=\frac{1}{4} ||\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}||^{2}-\frac{1}{4||\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{v}}||^{2}
$$
