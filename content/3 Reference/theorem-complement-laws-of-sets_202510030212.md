## Theorem

Let $A$ : Any [[3 Reference/def-set_202510022014\|set]]

Then
$$
\begin{align}
A \cup A^C & = U \\
A \cap A^C & = \emptyset \\
(A^C)^C & = A \\
U^C & = \emptyset \\
\emptyset^C & = U
\end{align}
$$
