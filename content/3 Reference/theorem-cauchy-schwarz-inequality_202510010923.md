## Theorem

Let
- $\mathbf{u}=(u_{1},u_{2},\dots,u_{n})$ vectors in $R^n$
- $\mathbf{v}=(v_{1},v_{2},\dots,v_{n})$ points vectors in $R^n$

Then
$$
|\mathbf{u}\cdot \mathbf{v}|\leq ||\mathbf{u}||\; ||\mathbf{v}||
$$

Or (in terms of components)
$$
u_{1}v_{1}+u_{2}v_{2}+\dots+u_{n}v_{n}\leq (u_{1}^{2}+u_{2}^{2}+\dots+u_{n}^{2})^{1/2}(v_{1}^{2}+v_{2}^{2}+\dots+u_{n}^{2})^{1/2}
$$
