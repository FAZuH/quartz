## Theorem

Let

- $\mathbf{u}=(u\_{1},u\_{2},\dots,u\_{n})$ vectors in $R^n$
- $\mathbf{v}=(v\_{1},v\_{2},\dots,v\_{n})$ points vectors in $R^n$

Then
$$
|\mathbf{u}\cdot \mathbf{v}|\leq ||\mathbf{u}||; ||\mathbf{v}||
$$

Or (in terms of components)
$$
u\_{1}v\_{1}+u\_{2}v\_{2}+\dots+u\_{n}v\_{n}\leq (u\_{1}^{2}+u\_{2}^{2}+\dots+u\_{n}^{2})^{1/2}(v\_{1}^{2}+v\_{2}^{2}+\dots+u\_{n}^{2})^{1/2}
$$
