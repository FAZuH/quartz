## Definition
**Odds** is the probability of success $\pi$ divided by the probability of failure $(1-\pi)$:
$$
\text{odds}=\frac{\pi}{1-\pi}
$$

## Remark

- The odds are nonnegative.
- Value greater than $1.0$ means a success is more likely than a failure.
- $\text{odds}=4.0$ means success is 4 times more likely than failure.


$$
\pi=\frac{\text{odds}}{\text{odds}+1}
$$
