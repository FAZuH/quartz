## About

A plot of the [[3 Reference/sample-autocorrelation_202603161400\|sample autocorrelation]] $r_k$ against the [[3 Reference/lag-definition_202603161400\|lag]] $k$. It is a key tool for identifying the structure of a time series process.

## Identifying MA vs AR using Correlogram

Key difference for model identification:
- MA: <u>Cuts off</u> cleanly at lag $q$
- AR: Decays exponentially
