## Notes

**From Claude**

The official QEMU documentation is the best primary source: https://www.qemu.org/docs/master/

For a more digestible intro, Arch Wiki's QEMU page is excellent and practical: https://wiki.archlinux.org/title/QEMU

The Arch Wiki page in particular is worth reading fully — it covers KVM, display backends, USB passthrough, networking, and disk images with concrete examples. Since you're on Arch it'll also be directly applicable without any distro-specific translation.
