## Definition

Let $V$ : Finite-dimensional [[def-vector-space-axioms_202509240715|vector space]] $V$

Then

- We call <u>number of vectors in a basis</u> for $V$ the **dimension** of $V$
- We denote **dimension** of $V$ as $\operatorname{dim}(V)$
- Additionally, the zero vector space is defined to have dimension $0$.
