## Theorem

Let $A,B,C$ be any [[3 Reference/def-set_202510022014\|set]]

Then
$$
\begin{align}
(A \cup B) \cup C &= A \cup (B \cup C) \\
(A \cap B) \cap C &= A \cap (B \cap C)
\end{align}
$$
