Rate based on **retrieval effort**, not how well you understand it after seeing the answer. <u>Only judge the moment of recall before revealing the card</u>.

> [!TIP]
> Decide on the difficulty first before revealing the card so you don't forget your decision.

## Rules

- **Again** — <u>forgot</u>, or recalled <u>wrong</u>
- **Hard** — recalled but it took <u>significant effort</u>
- **Good** — recalled correctly with <u>normal effort</u>
- **Easy** — recalled instantly with <u>zero effort</u>

Good and Easy is a bit vauge, so [[3 Reference/choosing-recall-difficulty-of-anki-cards_202605031807#Pratical rules]] help with deciding.

## Pratical rules

- <u>Most cards</u> should be **Good**.
- Use **Again** when you <u>fail</u>.
- Use **Hard** sparingly when you're right but it was a genuine <u>struggle</u>. 
- Use **Easy** only when it felt almost <u>insultingly obvious</u>.

Default to Good:
- If you're stuck between Hard and Good, pick Good.
- If stuck between Good and Easy, pick Good.

> [!TIP]
> Try not to obsess over the card description. The priority is the quality and speed of recalling the card. Other things are just noise.
