## Definition

[[3 Reference/def-state-stochastic_202603280801\|States]] that [[3 Reference/def-communication_202603280821\|communicate]] form a **class**.

> [!TIP] Interpretation
> Classes are equivalence classes under the communication relation. Two states in the same class can reach each other; states in different classes cannot.

## Properties

Classes are either:
- **Identical** (same class), or
- **Disjoint** (no overlap)
