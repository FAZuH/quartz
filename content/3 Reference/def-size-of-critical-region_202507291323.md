## Definition

if
$\alpha=\max_{\theta\in \omega_{0}}P_{\theta}[(X_{1},\dots,X_{n})\in C]$
Then

- We say [[def-test_202508050827|critical region]] $C$ is of **size** $\alpha$
- We often say $\alpha$ is the **significance level** of the [[def-test_202508050827|test]] associated with $C$
- The size/significance level $\alpha$ is the probability of making a [[def-test-error-types_202508050828|type I error]]
