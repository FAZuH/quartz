## Definition

Let $\{ e_{t} \}$ : [[white-noise_202603161400|White noise]]

A **random walk process** is defined as:

$$
\begin{align}
Y_{1} & = e_{1} \\
Y_{t} & = Y_{t-1} + e_{t}
\end{align}
$$

It can also be written as:

$$
Y_{t} = \sum_{i=1}^t e_{i}
$$

> [!tip]
> It is AR(1) with $\phi=1$

## Variance

$\text{Var}(X + Y) = \text{Var}(X) + \text{Var}(Y) + 2\text{Cov}(X,Y)$ .

White noise is independent, and identically distributed. So, $\text{Cov}(e_{s},e_{t}) = 0$, and $\text{Var}(e_{s} + e_{t}) = \text{Var}(e_{s}) + \text{Var}(e_{t}) = 2 \sigma^{2}_{e}$ .

From this property we can obtain

$$
\begin{align}
\text{Var}(Y_{t}) & = \text{Var}\left( \sum_{i=1}^{t} e_{i} \right) \\
 & = t\cdot \sigma^{2}_{e}
\end{align}
$$

$\blacksquare$
