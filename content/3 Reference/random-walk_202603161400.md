## Definition

Let ${ e\_{t} }$ : [[white-noise_202603161400|White noise]]

A **random walk process** is defined as:
$$
\begin{align}
Y\_{1} & = e\_{1} \\
Y\_{t} & = Y\_{t-1} + e\_{t}
\end{align}
$$

It can also be written as:
$$
Y\_{t} = \sum\_{i=1}^t e\_{i}
$$

> [!tip]
> It is AR(1) with $\phi=1$
