## Definition

Let $\{ e_{t} \}$ : [[3 Reference/white-noise_202603161400\|White noise]]

A **random walk process** is defined as:
$$
\begin{align}
Y_{1} & = e_{1} \\
Y_{t} & = Y_{t-1} + e_{t}
\end{align}
$$

It can also be written as:
$$
Y_{t} = \sum_{i=1}^t e_{i}
$$
> [!tip]
> It is AR(1) with $\phi=1$
