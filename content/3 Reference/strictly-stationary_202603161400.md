## Definition

A [[3 Reference/stochastic-process_202603161400\|stochastic process]] $\{ Y_{t} \}$ is **strictly stationary**

If for any $n\in \mathbb{Z}^+$, any set of time points $\{ t_{1},t_{2},\dots,t_{n} \}$, lag $k$ the joint cumulative distribution function of $(Y_{t_1}, Y_{t_2}, \dots, Y_{t_n})$ is identical to that of $(Y_{t_1+k}, Y_{t_2+k}, \dots, Y_{t_n+k})$

## Relationship with Weak Stationarity

If a [[3 Reference/stochastic-process_202603161400\|stochastic process]] $\{ Y_{t} \}$ is strictly stationary and $\operatorname{Var}(Y_{t})$ is finite for all $t$, then $\{ Y_{t} \}$ is also [[3 Reference/weakly-stationary_202603161400\|weakly stationary]].
