## Theorem

Let

- $c$ : [[Def-matrix|Scalar]]
- $A$ : [[Def-matrix|Matrix]]
- $0$ : [[def-zero-matrix_202509241049|Zero matrix]]

Suppose sizes of $A$ and $0$ are such that the operations can be performed

Then

- $A+0=0+A=A$
- $A-0=A$
- $A-A=A+(-A)=0$
- $0A=0$
- $cA=0\implies c=0\lor A=0$
