## Why is the model called simple?

It is called simple because there is only one predictor, $X$.

When there is more than one predictor, say $k$ predictors: $X\_1, X\_2, \dots, X\_k$, then the model is called multiple. See [[the-multiple-regression-model_202509091642|The Multiple Regression Model]].

## Interpretation of $β\_1$

When $x\_i$ is increased by 1 unit, there is a change (increase or decrease depends on the sign) of $β\_1$ unit in $y\_i$.
