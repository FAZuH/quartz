## Definition

Let $\mathbf{v}=(v_{1},v_{2},\dots,v_{n})$ vector in $R^n$

Then the **norm** of $\mathbf{v}$ is defined as

$$
||\mathbf{v}||=\sqrt{ v_{1}^{2}+v_{2}^{2}+\dots+v_{n}^{2} }
$$

## Remark

**Norm** of $\mathbf{v}$ is also often referred to as the **length** of $\mathbf{v}$ or the **magnitude** of $\mathbf{v}$.
