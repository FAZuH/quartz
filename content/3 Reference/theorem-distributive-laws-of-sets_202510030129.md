## Theorem

Let $A,B,C$ be any [[3 Reference/def-set_202510022014\|set]]

Then
$$
\begin{align}
A\cap(B\cup C) & = (A\cap B)\cup(A\cap C) \\
A\cup(B\cap C) & = (A\cup B)\cap(A\cup C)
\end{align}
$$
