A **latent variable** or **latent structure** is a concept in statistics and related fields that refers to an **unobserved** or **hidden** variable that isn’t directly measured but is inferred from the relationships among observed variables.

It’s like an underlying "cause" or "factor" that influences the data you can see, helping explain why certain patterns or correlations exist.

## Example

- **Observed**: Metrics like CPU usage, memory usage, disk I/O from Prometheus.
- **Latent Variable**: "System Load" — a hidden "stress" factor affecting all three metrics.
