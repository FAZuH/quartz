Suggestions sorted by ease of implementation:

## TIER 1 - Immediate, zero cost, minimal effort

1. **Start boring task immediately after dopamine-rich activity**
    
    - Finish coding session → immediately open boring assignment
    - Finish game/video → immediately do 5 minutes of work
    - Executive function still active, ride the momentum
    - No setup required, just timing
2. **"Just 5 minutes" rule**
    
    - Tell yourself you'll do 5 minutes only
    - Set timer, stop if you want after 5 minutes
    - Often continues once started (activation energy is the barrier)
    - Zero cost, instant implementation
3. **Identify minimum viable effort**
    
    - For each boring assignment: what's the minimum to pass?
    - Do only that, nothing more
    - Reduces overwhelming feeling
    - Just requires honest assessment
4. **Micro-deadlines**
    
    - Break assignment into smallest possible chunks
    - "By tonight: write outline" not "by next week: finish paper"
    - Creates more frequent urgency-dopamine hits
    - Just requires different framing of same work

## TIER 2 - Easy setup, free

5. **Public commitment**
    
    - Text friend: "I'll send you my draft by 8pm"
    - Post in Discord/group chat: "Doing X today"
    - External accountability without requiring their presence
    - One message, instant pressure
6. **Work in library/public space**
    
    - Physical presence of others provides structure
    - No arrangement needed, just go
    - Harder to get distracted with others around
    - Free, available now
7. **Timer for coding sessions**
    
    - Phone timer, 2-hour blocks
    - Place phone across room so you must get up
    - Prevents hyperfocus crashes
    - Already have phone, zero additional cost

## TIER 3 - Requires minimal setup/download

8. **Focusmate (free tier)**
    
    - 3 free sessions per week
    - 50-minute body doubling sessions
    - Webcam accountability with stranger
    - Sign up takes 5 minutes, first session available immediately
9. **Study Discord/Twitch streams**
    
    - Join existing study-with-me streams
    - Body doubling without interaction
    - Many available 24/7
    - Just search and join
10. **Work blocking app**
    
    - Cold Turkey (free), Freedom (paid but has free trial)
    - Block distracting sites during boring work
    - 10-minute setup
    - Free options available

## TIER 4 - Requires more effort/coordination

11. **Study group for boring subjects**
    
    - Regular meeting time forces you to do work
    - Social pressure + body doubling
    - Requires finding/organizing group
    - Scheduling coordination needed
12. **Scheduled meeting to discuss work**
    
    - Tell professor/TA: "Can I show you my draft on Tuesday?"
    - Forces completion before meeting
    - Requires asking someone (social friction)

## TIER 5 - Longer-term implementation

13. **Fix sleep schedule**
    
    - Most impactful, most difficult
    - Requires consistent effort over weeks
    - Start: shift bedtime 15 minutes earlier daily
    - Get sunlight immediately upon waking
    - Listed last because it's gradual, not immediate relief
