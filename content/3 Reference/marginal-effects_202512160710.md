The marginal effect of changing $x_{k}$:
- [[3 Reference/def-probit_202512160705\|Probit]]: $\Phi(x_{i}'\beta)\beta_{k}$
- [[3 Reference/def-logit_202512160701\|Logit]]: $\dfrac{\exp(x_{i}'\beta)}{[1+\exp(x_{i}'\beta)]^{2}}\beta_{k}$
- [[3 Reference/def-linear-probability-model-(lpm)_202512160705\|Linear Probability Model]]: $\beta_{k}$ if $x_{i}'\beta \in[0,1]$
