The marginal effect of changing $x\_{k}$:

- [[def-probit_202512160705|Probit]]: $\Phi(x\_{i}'\beta)\beta\_{k}$
- [[def-logit_202512160701|Logit]]: $\dfrac{\exp(x\_{i}'\beta)}{\[1+\exp(x\_{i}'\beta)]^{2}}\beta\_{k}$
- [[def-linear-probability-model-(lpm)_202512160705|Linear Probability Model]]: $\beta\_{k}$ if $x\_{i}'\beta \in\[0,1]$
