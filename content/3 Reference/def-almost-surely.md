Let
- $(\Omega,\mathcal{F}, P)$ : Probability space
- [[3 Reference/Def-events\|event]] $A\in \mathcal{F}$

If $P(A)=1$

Then $A$ happens **almost surely**

## Remark
$P(A)=1$ is equivalent to $P(A^C)=1$
