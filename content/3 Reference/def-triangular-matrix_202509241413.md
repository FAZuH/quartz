## Definition

A [[Def-matrix|square matrix]] in which all the [[Def-matrix|entries]] above the [[Def-matrix|main diagonal]] are zero is called **lower triangular** matrix.

A square matrix in which all the entries below the main diagonal are zero is called **upper triangular** matrix.

A [[Def-matrix|matrix]] that is either upper triangular or lower triangular is called **triangular** matrix.

## Example

$$
\begin{align}
\text{Upper Triangular Matrix} & :
\begin{bmatrix}
5 & 2 & 7 \\
0 & 3 & 1 \\
0 & 0 & 8
\end{bmatrix} \ \\

\text{Lower Triangular Matrix} & :
\begin{bmatrix}
4 & 0 & 0 \\
1 & 6 & 0 \\
3 & 2 & 9
\end{bmatrix}
\end{align}
$$
