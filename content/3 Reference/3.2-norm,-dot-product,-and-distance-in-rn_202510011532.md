## Exercise

### 1.a

> Find the [[3 Reference/def-norm_202510010836\|norm]] of $\mathbf{v}$, and a [[3 Reference/def-unit-vector_202510010840\|unit vector]] that is oppositely directed to $\mathbf{v}$.

Let $\mathbf{v}=(2,2,2)$

Norm of $\mathbf{v}$ is
$$
\begin{align}
||\mathbf{v}|| & = \sqrt{ 2^{2}+2^{2}+2^{2} } \\
 & = \sqrt{ 12 } \\
 & = 2\sqrt{ 3 }
\end{align}
$$

The unit vector of $\mathbf{v}$ is given by
$$
\begin{align}
\mathbf{u} & =  \frac{1}{||\mathbf{v}||} \mathbf{v}  \\
 & = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{ 3 }}(2,2,2) \\
 & = \left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 3 }}, \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 3 }}, \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 3 }} \right)
\end{align}
$$

The unit vector result can be confirmed using the property of unit vector,
$$
\begin{align}
||\mathbf{u}|| & = 1 \\
\left|\left|\left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}, \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}},\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)\right|\right| & = 1 \\
\sqrt{ \left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)^{2} }  & = 1 \\
\sqrt{ \frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{3} } & = 1 \\
\sqrt{ 1 } & = 1 \\
1 & = 1
\end{align}
$$

### 1.b

> Find the [[3 Reference/def-norm_202510010836\|norm]] of $\mathbf{v}$, and a [[3 Reference/def-unit-vector_202510010840\|unit vector]] that is oppositely directed to $\mathbf{v}$.

Let $\mathbf{v}=(1,0,2,1,3)$

$$
\begin{align}
||\mathbf{v}|| & = \sqrt{ 1^{2}+0^{2}+2^{2}+1^{2}+3^{2} }= \sqrt{ 15 } \\
\mathbf{u} & = \frac{1}{||\mathbf{v}||}\mathbf{v} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 15 }}(1,0,2,1,3) 
\end{align}
$$

## 2.a

> Find the [[3 Reference/def-norm_202510010836\|norm]] of $\mathbf{v}$, and a [[3 Reference/def-unit-vector_202510010840\|unit vector]] that is oppositely directed to $\mathbf{v}$.

Let $\mathbf{v}=(1,-1,2)$

$$
\begin{align}
||\mathbf{v}|| & = \sqrt{ 1^{2}+(-1)^{2}+2^{2} }= \sqrt{ 6 } = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{ 2 } \\
\mathbf{u} & = \frac{1}{||\mathbf{v}||}\mathbf{v} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 6 }}(1,-1,2) = \left( \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{ 2 },-\frac{1}{2} \sqrt{ 2 },\sqrt{ 2 } \right)
\end{align}
$$

## 2.b

> Find the [[3 Reference/def-norm_202510010836\|norm]] of $\mathbf{v}$, and a [[3 Reference/def-unit-vector_202510010840\|unit vector]] that is oppositely directed to $\mathbf{v}$.

Let $\mathbf{v}=(-2,3,3,-1)$

$$
\begin{align}

\mathbf{u} & = \frac{1}{||\mathbf{v}||}\mathbf{v} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 23 }}(-2,3,3,-1)
\end{align}
$$

## 3.a

> Evaluate the given expression with $\mathbf{u}=(2,-2,3),\mathbf{v}=(1-3,4)$, and $\mathbf{w}=(3,6,-4)$

$$
\begin{align}
||\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}|| & = ||(2,-2,3)+(1,-3,4)|| \\
 & = ||(3,-5,7)|| \\
 & = \sqrt{ 3^{2}+(-5)^{2}+7^{2} } \\
 & = \sqrt{ 83 }
\end{align}
$$
