## Definition

Let
- $A$ : [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|Matrix]]
- $c$ : [[3 Reference/Def-matrix#^def-scalar\|Scalar]]

Then 
- **Product** $cA$ is the matrix obtained by multiplying entry of $A$ by $c$
- We say $cA$ a **scalar multiple** of $A$
