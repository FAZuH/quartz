## Definition

Models where the mean function $\mu_t$ is a polynomial of time $t$.

### Linear Trend
$$
\mu_t = \beta_0 + \beta_1 t
$$

### Quadratic Trend
$$
\mu_t = \beta_0 + \beta_1 t + \beta_2 t^2
$$
