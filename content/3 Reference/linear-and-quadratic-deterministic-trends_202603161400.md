## Definition

Models where the mean function $\mu\_t$ is a polynomial of time $t$.

### Linear Trend

$$
\mu\_t = \beta\_0 + \beta\_1 t
$$

### Quadratic Trend

$$
\mu\_t = \beta\_0 + \beta\_1 t + \beta\_2 t^2
$$
