In a $2\times2$ contingency table, the **odds ratio** is defined as
 $$
\theta = \frac{\text{odds}_1}{\text{odds}_2} = \frac{\pi_1/(1-\pi_1)}{\pi_2/(1-\pi_2)}
$$
, where:
- $\text{odds}_{1}$ : The [[3 Reference/odds_202509221037\|odds]] of row 1
- $\text{odds}_{2}$ : The [[3 Reference/odds_202509221037\|odds]] of row 2
- $\pi_{1}$ : Probability of success of variable
