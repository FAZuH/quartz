## Definition

**Irrational numbers** $(\mathbb Q^C)$: Defined as real numbers not in $\mathbb Q$:
$$
\mathbb Q^C = \mathbb R - \mathbb Q
$$

> [!tip] The word "irrational" come from the fact that they cannot be expressed as "ratios" of integers.
