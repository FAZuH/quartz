## Definition

We define **[[3 Reference/def-power-of-a-test_202508050833\|power]] function** of a [[3 Reference/def-test_202508050827\|critical region]] to be 
$$ \gamma_{C}(\theta)=P_{\theta}[(X_{1},\dots,X_{n})\in C];\quad\theta\in \omega_{1} $$

Suppose $C_{1},C_{2}$ : Critical regions, both of [[3 Reference/def-size-of-critical-region_202507291323\|size]] $\alpha$

Then $C_{1}$ is better than $C_{2}$ if $\gamma_{C_{1}}(\theta)\geq \gamma_{C_{2}}(\theta), \forall \theta\in \omega_{1}$
