## Definition
If $$ A - B:=\{ x:x \in A\text{ and } x \not \in B \} $$

Then we say [[3 Reference/def-set_202510022014\|set]] $A- B$ is the **complement of $B$ relative to $A$**

## Remark

It is also often denoted by $A\setminus B$.
