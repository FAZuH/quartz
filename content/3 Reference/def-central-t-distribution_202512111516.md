## Definition

Let

- $W$ : [[Def-random-variable|Random variable]] from $N(\delta,1)$\[^1]
- $V$ : Random variable from $\chi^{2}(r)$\[^2]
- $W$ and $V$ be independent

Suppose
$$
T = \frac{W}{\sqrt{ V/r }}
$$

If $\delta = 0$

Then

- We say $T$ has a **central $t$-distribution**

> [!note]
> $T$ is said to have a noncentral $t$-distribution with $r$ degrees of freedom and noncentraility parameter $\delta$.
