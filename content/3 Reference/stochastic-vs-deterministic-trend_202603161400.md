## Definition

- **Deterministic Trend**: A trend that is a fixed function of time (e.g., linear $\mu_t = \beta_0 + \beta_1 t$).
- **Stochastic Trend**: A trend that arises from the accumulation of random shocks (e.g., a [[3 Reference/random-walk_202603161400\|random walk]]).
