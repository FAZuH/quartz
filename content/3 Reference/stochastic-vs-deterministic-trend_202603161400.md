## Definition

- **Deterministic Trend**: A trend that is a fixed function of time (e.g., linear $\mu\_t = \beta\_0 + \beta\_1 t$).
- **Stochastic Trend**: A trend that arises from the accumulation of random shocks (e.g., a random walk).
