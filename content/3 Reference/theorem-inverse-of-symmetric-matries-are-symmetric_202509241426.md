## Theorem

Let $A$ : [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|Matrix]]

If
- $A$ is [[3 Reference/def-inverse-matrix_202509241225\|invertible]]
- $A$ is [[3 Reference/def-symmetric-matrix_202509241413\|symmetric]]

Then $A^{-1}$ is symmetric
