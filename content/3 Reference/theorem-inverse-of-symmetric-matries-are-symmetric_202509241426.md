## Theorem

Let $A$ : [[Def-matrix|Matrix]]

If

- $A$ is [[def-inverse-matrix_202509241225|invertible]]
- $A$ is [[def-symmetric-matrix_202509241413|symmetric]]

Then $A^{-1}$ is symmetric
