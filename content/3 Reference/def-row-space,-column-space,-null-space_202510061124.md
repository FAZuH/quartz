## Definition

Let $A$ : $m\times n$ [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|matrix]]

Then
- [[3 Reference/def-subspace_202509240725\|Subspace]] of $R^n$ [[3 Reference/def-span_202509240805\|spanned]] by [[3 Reference/def-row-and-column-vector_202509240741\|row vectors]] of $A$ is called the **row space** of $A$
- Subspace of $R^m$ spanned by [[3 Reference/def-row-and-column-vector_202509240741\|column vectors]] of $A$ is called the **column space** of $A$
- [[3 Reference/def-solution-space_202510061127\|Solution space]] of [[3 Reference/Def-homogeneous-linear-system\|homogeneous system]] $A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{0}$ (which is subspace of $R^n$) is called the **null space** of $A$
