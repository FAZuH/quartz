## Theorem

Let $A$ : $n\times n$ [[Def-matrix|square matrix]]

Then the following statements are **equivalent**:

- (a) $A$ is [[theorem-matrix-invertibility_202509241243|invertible]]^a
- (b) $A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{0}$ has only the [[Def-trivial-solution|trivial solution]]^b
- (c) The [[Def-reduced-row-echelon-form-(rref)|reduced row echelon form]] of $A$ is $I\_{n}$^c
- (d) $A$ can be expressed as a product of [[def-elementary-matrix_202510021412|elementary matrices]]^d
- (e) $A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}$ is [[Def-consistency-of-linear-system|consistent]] for every $n\times 1$ matrix $\mathbf{b}$^e
- (f) $A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}$ has exactly one [[Def-solution-of-linear-system|solution]] for every $n\times 1$ matrix $\mathbf{b}$^f
- (g) $\det(A) \neq 0$^g
- (h) The [[def-row-and-column-vector_202509240741|column vectors]] of $A$ are [[def-linear-independence_202510060445|linearly independent]]^h
- (i) The [[def-row-and-column-vector_202509240741|row vectors]] of $A$ are linearly independent^i
- (j) The column vectors of $A$ [[def-span_202509240805|span]] $R^n$^j
- (k) The row vectors of $A$ span $R^n$^k
- (l) The column vectors of $A$ form a [[def-basis_202510061042|basis]] for $R^n$^l
- (m) The row vectors of $A$ for a basis for $R^n$^m
- (n) $A$ has [[def-rank-and-nullity_202510080316|rank]] $n$^n
- (o) $A$ has [[def-rank-and-nullity_202510080316|nullity]] $0$^o
- (p) The [[def-orthogonal_202510011638|orthogonal]] complement of the [[def-row-space,-column-space,-null-space_202510061124|null space]] of $A$ is $R^n$^p
- (q) The orthogonal complement of the [[def-row-space,-column-space,-null-space_202510061124|row space]] of $A$ is ${ \mathbf{0} }$^q
- (r) The [[def-kernel-and-range_202511050657|kernel]] of  is ${ \mathbf{0} }$^r
- (s) The [[def-kernel-and-range_202511050657|range]] of $T\_{A}$ is $R^n$^s
- (t) $T\_{A}$ is one-to-one^t
- (u) $\lambda=0$ is not an [[def-eigenvalue-eigenvector_202511050458|eigenvalue]] of $A$^u
