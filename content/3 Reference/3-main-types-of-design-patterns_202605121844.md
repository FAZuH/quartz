There are three main types of design patterns:

- **Creational**: Provide <u>object creation mechanisms</u> that increase flexibility and reuse of existing code. 
- **Structural**: Explain how to <u>assemble objects and classes into larger structures</u>, while keeping these structures flexible and efficient. 
- **Behavioral**: Take care of effective <u>communication</u> and the <u>assignment of responsibilities</u> between objects.
