## Definition

Let $A$ : [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|Square matrix]]

Then the **trace** of $A$, denoted by $\operatorname{tr}(A)$, is defined to be the sum of the [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|entries]] on the [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|main diagonal]] of $A$.

## Remark

The trace of $A$ is undefined if $A$ is not a square matrix.
