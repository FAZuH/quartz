## Definition

A [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|square matrix]] with $1$'s on the [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|main diagonal]] and $0$'s elsewhere is called an identity matrix.

Usually denoted as $I$, or if the sizes are important, $I_{n}$.

## Example

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
1
\end{bmatrix},\quad
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
0 & 1
\end{bmatrix},\quad
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$
