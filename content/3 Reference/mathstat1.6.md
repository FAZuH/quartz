## Definition: Support of discrete random variable
> Let
> - $X$ : Discrete random variable
> - $\mathcal{D}$ : Space of $X$
> 
> If
> $$
> \mathcal{S}_X = \{ x \in \mathcal{D} : P(X = x) > 0 \}
> $$
> 
> Then we say $\mathcal{S}_{X}$ is the **support** of $X$
