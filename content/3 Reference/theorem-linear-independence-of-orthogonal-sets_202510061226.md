## Theorem

If $S = \mathbf{v}_{1},\mathbf{v}_{2},\dots,\mathbf{v}_{n}$ is an [[def-orthogonal-and-orthonormal-sets_202510061213|Orthogonal set]] of nonzero vectors in an [[def-real-inner-product-space_202510061214|inner product space]]

Then $S$ is [[def-linear-independence_202510060445|linearly independent]]
