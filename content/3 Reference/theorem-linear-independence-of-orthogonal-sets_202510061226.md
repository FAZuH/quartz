## Theorem

If $S = \mathbf{v}_{1},\mathbf{v}_{2},\dots,\mathbf{v}_{n}$ is an [[3 Reference/def-orthogonal-and-orthonormal-sets_202510061213\|Orthogonal set]] of nonzero vectors in an [[3 Reference/def-real-inner-product-space_202510061214\|inner product space]]

Then $S$ is [[3 Reference/def-linear-independence_202510060445\|linearly independent]]
