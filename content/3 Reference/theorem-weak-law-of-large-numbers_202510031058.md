## Theorem

Let

- ${ X\_{n} }$ : Sequence of [[mathstat4.1#Definition 4.1.1 Random sample|random samples]], with
  - Common mean $\mu$
  - Common variance $\sigma^2<\infty$
- $$\bar{X}_{n}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^nX\_{i}$$

Then
$$
\bar{X}\_{n} \xrightarrow{P}\mu
$$
