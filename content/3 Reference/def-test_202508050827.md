## Definition
Let
- $X$ : [[3 Reference/Def-random-variable\|Random variable]] wth [[3 Reference/Def-probability-density-function-(pdf)\|pdf]] $f(x;\theta),\theta\in \Omega$
- $X_{1},\dots,X_{n}$ : [[3 Reference/Def-random-sample\|Random sample]] of $X$
- $\mathcal{D}=\operatorname{space}\{(X_{1},\dots,X_{n})\}$ : [[3 Reference/mathstat1.1#Definition Sample space\|Sample space]]

Suppose
- $H_{0}$ : Null [[3 Reference/def-hypothesis_202508050806\|hypothesis]]
- $H_{1}$ : Alternative [[3 Reference/def-hypothesis_202508050806\|hypothesis]]

Then
- A **test** of $H_{0}$ versus $H_{1}$ is based on a [[3 Reference/def-subset_202507240733\|subset]] $C\subset \mathcal{D}$
- We call $C$ the **critical region**
- **Decision rule** (test) of $C$ is
$$
\begin{array}{rl}
\text{Reject }H_{0}\; (\text{Accept }H_{1}) & \text{if } (X_{1},\dots,X_{n}) \in C \\
\text{Retain }H_{0}\; (\text{Reject }H_{1}) & \text{if } (X_{1},\dots,X_{n}) \in C^c
\end{array}
$$
