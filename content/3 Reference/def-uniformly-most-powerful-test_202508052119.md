## Definition

Let $C$ : [[3 Reference/def-uniformly-most-powerful-critical-region_202508052119\|UMP critical region]] of [[3 Reference/def-size-of-critical-region_202507291323\|size]] $\alpha$ for testing the [[3 Reference/def-types-of-statistical-hypotheses_202508051003\|simple hypothesis]] $H_{0}$ against an  [[3 Reference/def-types-of-statistical-hypotheses_202508051003\|alternative composite hypothesis]] $H_{1}$

A [[3 Reference/def-test_202508050827\|test]] defined by $C$ is called a **uniformly most powerful (UMP) test**, with [[3 Reference/def-size-of-critical-region_202507291323\|significance level]] $\alpha$, for testing the simple hypothesis $H_{0}$ against the alternative composite hypothesis $H_{1}$.
