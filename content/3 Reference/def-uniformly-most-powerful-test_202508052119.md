## Definition

Let $C$ : [[def-uniformly-most-powerful-critical-region_202508052119|UMP critical region]] of [[def-size-of-critical-region_202507291323|size]] $\alpha$ for testing the [[def-types-of-statistical-hypotheses_202508051003|simple hypothesis]] $H_{0}$ against an  alternative [[def-types-of-statistical-hypotheses_202508051003|composite hypothesis]] $H_{1}$

A [[def-test_202508050827|test]] defined by $C$ is called a **uniformly most powerful (UMP) test**, with [[def-size-of-critical-region_202507291323|significance level]] $\alpha$, for testing the simple hypothesis $H_{0}$ against the alternative composite hypothesis $H_{1}$.
