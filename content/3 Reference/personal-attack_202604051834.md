## Definition

Let:
- $P$ : A proposition
- $S$ : The person or group claiming $P$

Then an argument commits the **Personal Attack** (Ad Hominem) if and only if it asserts:
$$
\text{I have a problem with } S \implies P \text{ is false}
$$
