## Definition

Let 
- $X$ : [[3 Reference/Def-random-variable\|Random variable]] with [[3 Reference/Def-probability-density-function-(pdf)\|pdf]]/[[3 Reference/Def-probability-mass-function-(pmf)\|pmf]] $f(x;\theta), \theta\in \Omega$
- $\mathbf{X}'=(X_{1},\dots,X_{n})$ : [[3 Reference/Def-random-sample\|Random sample]] on $X$

Consider 
- [[3 Reference/def-hypothesis_202508050806\|Hypotheses]] $H_{0}: \theta \in \omega_{0}$ versus $H_{1}: \theta\in \omega_{1}$
- A [[3 Reference/def-test_202508050827\|test]] with [[3 Reference/def-test_202508050827\|critical region]] $C$ and [[3 Reference/def-size-of-critical-region_202507291323\|significance level]] $\alpha$

If
$$ P_{\theta}(\mathbf{X}\in C) \geq \alpha, \quad \forall \theta \in \omega_{1} $$

Then we say that this test is **unbiased**
