## Theorem

Let *$a,b,u,z \in \mathbb R$*

1. $(z + a = a) \Rightarrow \textcolor{red}{z = 0}$
2. $(b\neq 0 \wedge u\cdot b = b) \Rightarrow \textcolor{red}{u = 1}$
3. $\textcolor{red}{a \cdot 0 = 0}$
