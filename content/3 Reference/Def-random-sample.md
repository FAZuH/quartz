## Definition

Let $X\_{1},\dots, X\_{n}$ : [[Def-random-sample|Random samples]] of size $n$

If $X\_{1},\dots, X\_{n}$ are [[Def-independent-events|independent]] and identically distributed (iid)

Then we say $X\_{1},\dots, X\_{n}$ are **random samples**

## Remark

By "_identically distributed_" we mean that all of the $X\_{i}$s have the same distribution.
