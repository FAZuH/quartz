## Definition

Let $A$ : [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|Square matrix]]

Then
$$
\begin{align}
A^0 & = I \\
A^n & = AA\dots A\quad \text{[n factors]}
\end{align}
$$

If $A$ is [[3 Reference/def-inverse-matrix_202509241225\|invertible]]

Then
$$
A^{-n} = (A^{-1})^n=A^{-1}A^{-1}\dots A^{-1}\quad\text{[n factosr]}
$$
