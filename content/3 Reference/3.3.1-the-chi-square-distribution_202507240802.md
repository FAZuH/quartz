## Corollary 3.3.1

Let $X_{1},\dots,X_{n}$ : Independent [[3 Reference/Def-random-variable\|random variables]]

Suppose $X_{i}\sim \chi^2(r_{i}),\forall i=1,\dots,n$

Then 
$$ Y = \sum_{i=1}^nX_{i} \sim \chi^2\left( \sum_{i=1}^nr_{i} \right) $$
