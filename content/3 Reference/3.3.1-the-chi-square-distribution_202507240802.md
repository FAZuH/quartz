## Corollary 3.3.1

Let $X\_{1},\dots,X\_{n}$ : Independent [[Def-random-variable|random variables]]

Suppose $X\_{i}\sim \chi^2(r\_{i}),\forall i=1,\dots,n$

Then
$$ Y = \sum\_{i=1}^nX\_{i} \sim \chi^2\left( \sum\_{i=1}^nr\_{i} \right) $$
