## Theorem

Let $R$ : [[Def-reduced-row-echelon-form-(rref)|Reduced Row Echelon Form (RREF)]] of matrix of size $n\times n$.

Then either

1. $R$ has row of $0$'s, or
2. $R$ is the [[def-identity-matrix_202509241204|identity matrix]] $I_{n}$
