## Theorem

> Let:
> - $X$ random variable
> - $m \in \mathbb{P}$
> - $k\in \mathbb{P}, \quad k\leq m$
> 
> If $E(X^m)$ exists, then $E(X^k)$ exists

If a higher-order moment exists, then all lower-order moments must also exist
