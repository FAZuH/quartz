## Definition

Let

- $X\_1, \dots, X\_n$ : [[Def-random-sample|Random sample]] on $X$ with [[Def-probability-density-function-(pdf)|pdf]]/[[Def-probability-mass-function-(pmf)|pmf]] of $X$ is a [[def-regular-exponential-class-on-random-vectors_202507171021|regular case of the exponential class]]
- $$ Y\_j = \sum\_{i=1}^n K\_j(X\_i), \quad j=1, \dots, m $$
  [[def-jointly-sufficient-statistic_202507171021|jointly sufficient statistics]] for parameters $\theta\_{1},\dots,\theta\_{m}$

If $n>m$

Then

- The family of [[Def-probability-density-function-(pdf)|pdfs]] of these $Y\_1, Y\_2, \dots, Y\_m$ is [[def-complete-family-of-pdf-pmf_202507170933|complete]].
- We refer to $Y\_1, Y\_2, \dots, Y\_m$ as **joint complete sufficient statistics** for $\theta\_{1},\dots,\theta\_{m}$
