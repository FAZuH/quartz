## Theorem

Let $\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v},\mathbf{w}$ vectors in $R^n$

Then
- $||\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v} \leq ||\mathbf{u}|| + ||\mathbf{v}||$ ([[3 Reference/theorem-triangle-inequality_202508160051\|Triangle inequality]] for vectors)
- $d(\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v})\leq d(\mathbf{u},\mathbf{w})+d(\mathbf{w},\mathbf{v})$ ( Triangle inequality for distances)
