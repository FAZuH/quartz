## About

In many applications, particularly in business and economics, the assumption of stationarity (constant mean and variance) is unrealistic.

- Trends are often stochastic rather than deterministic.
- Variance may increase over time.
- Differencing provides a way to transform these non-stationary series into stationary ones for modeling.
