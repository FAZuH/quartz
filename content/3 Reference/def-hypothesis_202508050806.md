## Definition

Let $X$ : [[Def-random-variable|Random variable]] with [[Def-probability-density-function-(pdf)|pdf]] : $f(x;\theta), \theta\in \Omega$

Suppose due to theory/preliminary experiment

- $w\_{0}, w\_{1}$ disjoint subsets of $\Omega$, (i.e., $w\_{0}\cup w\_{1}=\Omega$)
- $\theta \in \omega\_{0}$ or $\theta \in \omega\_{1}$

Then

- We label these **hypotheses** as
  $$
  \begin{align}
  H\_{0} & : \theta\in \omega\_{0} \\
  H\_{1} & : \theta\in \omega\_{1}
  \end{align}
  $$
- We refer the hypothesis $H\_{0}$ as the **null hypothesis**
- We refer the hypothesis $H\_{1}$ as the **alternative hypothesis**
