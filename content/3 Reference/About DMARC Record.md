DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting, and Conformance) is a mechanism that kind of ties [SPF](About%20SPF%20Record.md) and [DKIM](About%20DKIM%20Record.md), and allows domain owners to tell "Receiving Mail Server" how to handle emails that claim to come from a "Sending Mail Server", but did not align with SPF or DKIM.
