## Theorem

Let

- $E$ : $m\times m$ [[def-elementary-matrix_202510021412|elementary matrix]] from performing certain [[theorem-elementary-row-operation_202510021405|elementary row operation]] on [[def-identity-matrix_202509241204|identity matrix]] $I_{m}$
- $A$ : $m\times m$ [[Def-matrix|matrix]]

Then the [[Def-matrix-product|product]] $EA$ is the matrix that results when the same row operation (that is performed on $I_{m}$) is performed on $A$.
