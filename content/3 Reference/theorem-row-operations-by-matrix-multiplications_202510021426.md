## Theorem

Let
- $E$ : $m\times m$ [[3 Reference/def-elementary-matrix_202510021412\|elementary matrix]] from performing certain [[3 Reference/theorem-elementary-row-operation_202510021405\|elementary row operation]] on [[3 Reference/def-identity-matrix_202509241204\|identity matrix]] $I_{m}$
- $A$ : $m\times m$ [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|matrix]]

Then the [[3 Reference/Def-matrix-product\|product]] $EA$ is the matrix that results when the same row operation (that is performed on $I_{m}$) is performed on $A$.
