## Theorem

If the sizes of the matrices are such that the stated operations can be performed, then

1. $(A^T)^T=A$
2. $(A\pm B)^{T}=A^{T}\pm B^{T}$
3. $(kA)^{T}=kA^{T}$
4. $(AB)^{T}=B^{T}A^{T}$
5. $(A\_{1}A\_{2}\dots A\_{n})^{T}=A\_{n}^{T}\dots A\_{2}^{T}A\_{1}^{T}$
