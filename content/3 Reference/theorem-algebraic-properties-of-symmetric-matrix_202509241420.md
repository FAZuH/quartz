- $A,B$ : [[3 Reference/def-symmetric-matrix_202509241413\|Symmetric matrices]] of same size
- $k$ : Any [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|scalar]]

Then $A^{T}$, $A\pm B$ and $kA$ is symmetric
