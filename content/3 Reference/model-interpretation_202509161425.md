## Why is the model called simple?

It is called simple because there is only one predictor, $X$.

When there is more than one predictor, say $k$ predictors: $X_1, X_2, \dots, X_k$, then the model is called multiple. See [[3 Reference/the-multiple-regression-model_202509091642\|The Multiple Regression Model]].

## What is the interpretation of $β_1$ in this model?

When $x_i$ is increased by 1 unit, there is a change (increase or decrease depends on the sign) of $β_1$ unit in $y_i$.
