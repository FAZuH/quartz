## Theorem

The $2\times 2$ [[3 Reference/Def-matrix\|matrix]] 
$$
A = \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}
$$
is [[3 Reference/def-inverse-matrix_202509241225\|invertible]] if and only if $ad−bc \neq 0$, in which case the inverse is given by the formula
$$
\begin{align}
A^{-1} & = \det(A)\begin{bmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{bmatrix} \\
 & = \frac{1}{ad-bc} \begin{bmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{bmatrix}
\end{align}
$$
