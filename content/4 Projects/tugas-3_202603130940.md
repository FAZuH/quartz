## Question

Suppose that $Y_t = A + Bt + X_t$, where $\{X_t\}$ is a random walk. First suppose that $A$ and $B$ are constants.
- a. Is $\{Y_t\}$ stationary?
- b. Is $\{\nabla Y_t\}$ stationary?

Now suppose that $A$ and $B$ are random variables that are independent of the random walk $\{X_t\}$.
- c. Is $\{Y_t\}$ stationary?
- d. Is $\{\nabla Y_t\}$ stationary?

## Answer

### a.

> [!NOTE]
> Quote from [[1_Pertemuan 1a.pdf]] page 35:
> 
> "Dalam kuliah ini, istilah stasioner jika digunakan sendiri akan selalu mengacu pada bentuk stasioneritas yang lebih lemah"

Suatu [[2 Personal/rangkuman_202603011304#Definition Stochastic Process\|stochastic process]] $\{ Z_{t} \}$ dikatakan [[2 Personal/rangkuman_202603011304#Definition Weakly Stationary\|weakly stationary]] apabila:
1.  $E[Z_t] = \mu$   (mean konstan)
2.  $\operatorname{Var}(Z_t) < \infty,\quad\forall t$   (variansi ada)
3.  $\operatorname{Cov}(Z_t, Z_{t-k}) = \gamma_k$    (cov independen dari $t$)

Akan dicari apakah $Y_{t} = A + Bt + X_{t}$ dimana $\{ X_{t} \}$ adalah random walk dan $A$ dan $B$ konstan.

Diketahui dari sifat random walk[^1] bahwa $E[X_{t}]=0$.

Sehingga,
$$
\begin{align}
Y_{t} & = A + Bt + X_{t} \\ \\

E[Y_{t}] & = E\left[ A + Bt + X_{t} \right]\\
 & = A + Bt + E[X_{t}] \\
 & = A + Bt 
\end{align}
$$

$\therefore$ Karena $E[Y_{t}]$ berbeda untuk setiap $t$, maka $Y_{t}$ tidak stasioner

### b. 

Akan dicari apakah $\nabla Y_{t} = Y_{t} - Y_{t-1}$ stasioner:

$$
\begin{align}
\nabla Y_{t} & = Y_{t} - Y_{t-1} \\
 & = (A+Bt+X_t) - (A+B(t-1)+X_{t-1}) \\
  & = B + e_t \\ \\

E[\nabla Y_{t}] & = E[B + e_{t}] \\
 & = B\quad\quad (\text{konstan }\forall t) \\ \\

\operatorname{Var}(\nabla Y_t) &= \operatorname{Var}(B + e_t)  \\
&= \operatorname{Var}(e_t) \\
&= \sigma_e^2 <\infty,\quad\quad\forall t \\ \\

\operatorname{Cov}(\nabla Y_t, \nabla Y_{t-k}) &= \operatorname{Cov}(B+e_t,\ B+e_{t-k})  \\
 &= \operatorname{Cov}(e_t, e_{t-k})  \\
  &= 0 
\end{align}
$$

$\therefore$ Terbukti $\nabla Y_{t}$ adalah stasioner.

### c.

Andaikan $A$ dan $B$ variabel acak yang independen terhadap $\{ X_{t} \}$

Dapat diperoleh:
$$
\begin{align}
Y_{t} & = A + Bt + X_{t} \\
\\
E[Y_{t}] & = E\left[ A + Bt + X_{t} \right]\\
& = E[A] + t\,E[B]
\end{align}
$$

$\therefore$ $\{ Y_{t} \}$ tidak stasioner, karena $E[Y_{t}]$ konstan jika dan hanya jika $E[B] = 0$.

### d.

Dapat diperoleh:
$$
\begin{align}
\nabla Y_{t} & = Y_{t} - Y_{t-1} \\
& = (A+Bt+X_t) - (A+B(t-1)+X_{t-1}) \\
& = B + e_t \\
\\
E[\nabla Y_{t}] & = E[B + e_{t}] \\
& = E[B] \quad\quad (\text{konstan }\forall t) \\
\\
\operatorname{Var}(\nabla Y_t) &= \operatorname{Var}(B + e_t) \\
&= \operatorname{Var}(B) + \operatorname{Var}(e_t) \\
&= \operatorname{Var}(B) + \sigma_e^2 < \infty \quad\quad \forall t \\
\\
\operatorname{Cov}(\nabla Y_t, \nabla Y_{t-k}) &= \operatorname{Cov}(B+e_t,\ B+e_{t-k}) \\
&= \operatorname{Var}(B) + \operatorname{Cov}(e_t, e_{t-k}) \\
&= \operatorname{Var}(B) \quad (k \neq 0)
\end{align}
$$

$\therefore$ Ketiga syarat terpenuhi: mean $E[B]$ konstan, variansi $\operatorname{Var}(B) + \sigma_e^2 < \infty$, dan kovarians hanya bergantung pada $k$ . Maka $\{\nabla Y_t\}$ stasioner.
