### 3

> For the sample $(y_{i},x_{i})$, $i=1,\dots,N$ and the linear regression $y_{i}=\beta_{1}+\beta_{2}x_{i}+\epsilon_{i}$
> 1. Write out the matrices $X'X$ and $X'y$
> 2. Write the OLS estimator $b=(X'X)^{-1}X'y$

Diketahui:
- $\epsilon_{i}\sim N(0,\sigma^{2}),\quad\forall i$
- $y_{i}=\beta_{1}+\beta_{2}x_{i}+\epsilon_{i}$

Misal
$$
\begin{align}
X & = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & x_{1} \\
\vdots & \vdots \\
1 & x_{N}
\end{bmatrix} \\

\beta & = \begin{bmatrix}
\beta_{1} \\
\beta_{2}
\end{bmatrix} \\
 
\epsilon & = \begin{bmatrix}
\epsilon_{1} \\
\vdots \\
\epsilon_{N}
\end{bmatrix} \\

Y & = \begin{bmatrix}
y_{1} \\
\vdots \\
y_{N}
\end{bmatrix}

\end{align}
$$

Maka,
$$
\begin{align}
X'X & = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & \dots & 1 \\
x_{1} & \dots & x_{N}
\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}
1 & x_{1} \\
\vdots & \vdots \\
1 & x_{N}
\end{bmatrix} \\
 & = \begin{bmatrix}
N & \sum_{i}^Nx_{i} \\
\sum_{i}^Nx_{i} & \sum_{i}^Nx_{i}^{2}
\end{bmatrix} \\
X'Y & = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & \dots & 1 \\
x_{1} & \dots & x_{N}
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
y_{1} \\
\vdots \\
y_{N}
\end{bmatrix} \\
 & = \begin{bmatrix}
\sum_{i}^Ny_{i} \\
\sum_{i}^Nx_{i}y_{i}
\end{bmatrix}
\end{align}
$$

2. 

Peroleh invers:
$$
\begin{align}
(X'X)^{-1} & = \begin{bmatrix}
N & \sum_{i}^Nx_{i} \\
\sum_{i}^Nx_{i} & \sum_{i}^Nx_{i}^2
\end{bmatrix}^{-1} \\
 & = \frac{1}{N\cdot \sum_{i}^Nx_{i}^{2} - (\sum_{i}^Nx_{i})^{2} } \begin{bmatrix}
\sum_{i}^Nx_{i}^{2} & -\sum_{i}^Nx_{i} \\
-\sum_{i}^Nx_{i} & N
\end{bmatrix}
\end{align}
$$

Maka
$$
\begin{align}
b & = (X'X)^{-1}X'Y \\
 & = \frac{1}{N\cdot \sum_{i}^Nx_{i}^{2} - \left( \sum_{i}^Nx_{i} \right)^{2} } \begin{bmatrix}
\sum_{i}^Nx_{i}^{2} & -\sum_{i}^Nx_{i} \\
-\sum_{i}^Nx_{i} & N
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
\sum_{i}^Ny_{i} \\
\sum_{i}^Nx_{i}y_{i}
\end{bmatrix} \\
 & = \frac{1}{N\cdot \sum_{i}^Nx_{i}^{2} - \left( \sum_{i}^Nx_{i} \right)^{2} }
\begin{bmatrix}
\sum x_{i}^{2}\sum y_{i} - \sum x_{i}\sum x_{i}y_{i} \\
-\sum x_{i}\sum y_{i} + N \sum x_{i}y_{i}
\end{bmatrix} \\
 
 \begin{bmatrix}
b_{1} \\
b_{2}
\end{bmatrix} & = \begin{bmatrix}
 
 \dfrac{\sum x_{i}^{2}\sum y_{i} - \sum x_{i}\sum x_{i}y_{i}}{N\cdot \sum x_{i}^{2} - \left( \sum x_{i} \right)^{2} } \\

\dfrac{ -\sum x_{i}\sum y_{i} + N \sum x_{i}y_{i} }{N\cdot \sum x_{i}^{2} - \left( \sum x_{i} \right)^{2} } 

\end{bmatrix}
\end{align}
$$

### 4

> For the simple regression $y_{i}=\alpha+\beta x_{i}+\epsilon_{i},i=1,\dots,N$, show that the variance of the OLS estimate for $\beta$ is $\sigma^{2}/Ns_{x}^{2}$, where $\operatorname{Var}(\epsilon)=\sigma^{2}$ and $\operatorname{Var}(x_{i})=s_{x}^{2}$

Akan ditunjukkan $\operatorname{Var}(b) = \sigma^{2}/Ns_{x}^{2} = \operatorname{Var}(\epsilon)/N\operatorname{Var}(x_{i})$

Dari hasil sebelumnya,

$$b_2 = \frac{N\sum x_i y_i - \sum x_i \sum y_i}{N\sum x_i^2 - (\sum x_i)^2}$$

Diketahui
$$
\begin{align}
\bar{x} & = \frac{1}{N}\sum x \\
\bar{y} & = \frac{1}{N}\sum y
\end{align}
$$

Perhatikan bahwa
$$
\begin{align}

\sum (x_i - \bar{x})(y_i - \bar{y}) & = \sum x_i y_i - N\bar{x}\bar{y} \\

\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2 & = \sum x_i^2 - N\bar{x}^2

\end{align}
$$


Jadi,
$$b_2 = \frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})(y_i - \bar{y})}{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}$$

Substitisukan $y_i = \alpha + \beta x_i + \epsilon_i$, sehingga diperoleh:

$$
\begin{align} \\
b_2 &= \frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})(\alpha + \beta x_i + \epsilon_i - \bar{y})}{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2} \\
&= \frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})(\beta x_i + \epsilon_i - \beta\bar{x} - \bar{\epsilon})}{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2} \\
&= \frac{\beta\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2 + \sum (x_i - \bar{x})(\epsilon_i - \bar{\epsilon})}{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2} \\
&= \beta + \frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})(\epsilon_i - \bar{\epsilon})}{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}
\end{align}
$$

Karena $\sum(x_{i}-\bar{x})=\sum x_{i}- N\bar{x}=\sum x_{i}-\sum x_{i}=0$ maka $\sum (x_i - \bar{x})\bar{\epsilon} = \bar{\epsilon}\sum(x_i - \bar{x}) = 0$, sehingga,

$$b_2 = \beta + \frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})\epsilon_i}{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}$$

Jadi,
$$
\begin{align}
\operatorname{Var}(b_2) &= \operatorname{Var}\left(\frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})\epsilon_i}{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}\right) \\
&= \frac{1}{[\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2]^2}\operatorname{Var}\left(\sum (x_i - \bar{x})\epsilon_i\right)
\end{align}
$$

dengan
$$
\begin{align}
\operatorname{Var}\left(\sum (x_i - \bar{x})\epsilon_i\right) &= \sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2 \operatorname{Var}(\epsilon_i) \\
&= \sigma^2 \sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2 
\end{align}
$$

Sehingga
$$\operatorname{Var}(b_2) = \frac{\sigma^2 \sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{[\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2]^2} = \frac{\sigma^2}{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}$$

Karena $s_x^2 = \operatorname{Var}(x_i) = \frac{1}{N}\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2$, dapat diperoleh $\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2 = Ns_x^2$.

Jadi, $$\operatorname{Var}(b_2) = \frac{\sigma^2}{Ns_x^2}$$


## Exercise 2.1 (regression) buku

> Consider the following linear regression model:
> $$ y_{i}=\beta_{1}+\beta_{2}x_{i2}+\beta_{3}x_{i3}+\epsilon_{i}=x_{i}'\beta+\epsilon_{i} $$

### a

> a. Explain how the ordinary least squares estimator for $\beta$ is determined, and derive an expression for $b$

Estimator OLS untuk $\beta$, didapat dengan meminimalkan SSE:
$$
S(\beta) = \sum_{i=1}^N (y_{i}-x_{i}'\beta)
$$

Dengan menurunkan $S(\beta)=0$, diperoleh
$$
\begin{align}
S'(\beta) = -2 \sum_{i=1}^N x_{i}(y_{i}-x_{i}'b)  & = 0 \\
\sum_{i=1}^N (x_{i}y_{i} - x_{i}x_{i}'b) & = 0 \\
\sum_{i=1}^N x_{i}y_{i} & = \sum_{i=1}^Nx_{i}x_{i}'b
\end{align}
$$

Asumsikan bahwa $\sum_{i=1}^N x_{i}x'_{i}$ adalah matriks invertibel. Maka dapat diperoleh OLS estimator untuk $\beta$, yang dinotasikan dengan $b$
$$
b = \left( \sum_{i=1}^N x_{i}'x_{i} \right)^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^Nx_{i}y_{i}
$$

### b.

> Which assumptions to make $b$ an unbiased estimator for $\beta$?

1. $E[\epsilon_{i}]=0,\quad\forall i$
2. $E[\epsilon_{i}|x_{i}]=0,\quad\forall i$

### c.

> Explain how a confidence interval for $\beta_{2}$ can be constructed. Which additional assumptions are needed?

Dengan asumsi A1-5,
$$
\begin{align}
b_{i}  & \sim N(\beta_{i},\sigma^{2}(X'X)^{-1}) \\
\frac{b_{i}-\beta_{i}}{\sqrt{ \sigma^{2}(X'X)^{-1}}} & \sim N(0,1)
\end{align}
$$

Diketahui $E[s^{2}]=\sigma^{2}$ dengan $s^{2}=\frac{1}{N-k}\sum\hat{\epsilon}_{i}^{2}$. Maka,
$$
SE(\beta_{i})=\frac{b_{i}-\beta_{i}}{\sqrt{ \sigma^{2}(X'X)^{-1}}} \sim t_{N-k}
$$

Jadi,
$$ b_{i} \pm t_{\alpha/2;N-k}\cdot SE(b_{i}) $$


### d.

> Explain how one can test the hypothesis that $\beta_{3}=1$

Hipotesis:
- $H_{0}$ : $\beta_{3}=1$
- $H_{1}$ : $\beta_{3}\neq1$

Statistik uji:
$$
\begin{align}
t & = \frac{b_{i}-\beta_{3}}{SE(b_{i})}\sim t_{N-k} \\
& = \frac{b_{3}-1}{SE(b_{3})}\sim t_{N-3}
\end{align}
$$

Perhatikan bahwa dibawah A1-A4, $t_{N-k}\xrightarrow{D}z$

Tolak $H_{0}$, apabila $|t|>z_{\alpha/2}$
