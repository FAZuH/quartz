### 3

> For the sample $(y\_{i},x\_{i})$, $i=1,\dots,N$ and the linear regression $y\_{i}=\beta\_{1}+\beta\_{2}x\_{i}+\epsilon\_{i}$
>
> 1. Write out the matrices $X'X$ and $X'y$
> 2. Write the OLS estimator $b=(X'X)^{-1}X'y$

Diketahui:

- $\epsilon\_{i}\sim N(0,\sigma^{2}),\quad\forall i$
- $y\_{i}=\beta\_{1}+\beta\_{2}x\_{i}+\epsilon\_{i}$

Misal
$$
\begin{align}
X & = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & x\_{1} \\
\vdots & \vdots \\
1 & x\_{N}
\end{bmatrix} \\

\beta & = \begin{bmatrix}
\beta\_{1} \\
\beta\_{2}
\end{bmatrix} \\

\epsilon & = \begin{bmatrix}
\epsilon\_{1} \\
\vdots \\
\epsilon\_{N}
\end{bmatrix} \\

Y & = \begin{bmatrix}
y\_{1} \\
\vdots \\
y\_{N}
\end{bmatrix}

\end{align}
$$

Maka,
$$
\begin{align}
X'X & = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & \dots & 1 \\
x\_{1} & \dots & x\_{N}
\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}
1 & x\_{1} \\
\vdots & \vdots \\
1 & x\_{N}
\end{bmatrix} \\
& = \begin{bmatrix}
N & \sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i} \\
\sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i} & \sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i}^{2}
\end{bmatrix} \\
X'Y & = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & \dots & 1 \\
x\_{1} & \dots & x\_{N}
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
y\_{1} \\
\vdots \\
y\_{N}
\end{bmatrix} \\
& = \begin{bmatrix}
\sum\_{i}^Ny\_{i} \\
\sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i}y\_{i}
\end{bmatrix}
\end{align}
$$

2.

Peroleh invers:
$$
\begin{align}
(X'X)^{-1} & = \begin{bmatrix}
N & \sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i} \\
\sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i} & \sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i}^2
\end{bmatrix}^{-1} \\
& = \frac{1}{N\cdot \sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i}^{2} - (\sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i})^{2} } \begin{bmatrix}
\sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i}^{2} & -\sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i} \\
-\sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i} & N
\end{bmatrix}
\end{align}
$$

Maka
$$
\begin{align}
b & = (X'X)^{-1}X'Y \\
& = \frac{1}{N\cdot \sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i}^{2} - \left( \sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i} \right)^{2} } \begin{bmatrix}
\sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i}^{2} & -\sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i} \\
-\sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i} & N
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
\sum\_{i}^Ny\_{i} \\
\sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i}y\_{i}
\end{bmatrix} \\
& = \frac{1}{N\cdot \sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i}^{2} - \left( \sum\_{i}^Nx\_{i} \right)^{2} }
\begin{bmatrix}
\sum x\_{i}^{2}\sum y\_{i} - \sum x\_{i}\sum x\_{i}y\_{i} \\
-\sum x\_{i}\sum y\_{i} + N \sum x\_{i}y\_{i}
\end{bmatrix} \\

\begin{bmatrix}
b\_{1} \\
b\_{2}
\end{bmatrix} & = \begin{bmatrix}

\dfrac{\sum x\_{i}^{2}\sum y\_{i} - \sum x\_{i}\sum x\_{i}y\_{i}}{N\cdot \sum x\_{i}^{2} - \left( \sum x\_{i} \right)^{2} } \\

\dfrac{ -\sum x\_{i}\sum y\_{i} + N \sum x\_{i}y\_{i} }{N\cdot \sum x\_{i}^{2} - \left( \sum x\_{i} \right)^{2} }

\end{bmatrix}
\end{align}
$$

### 4

> For the simple regression $y\_{i}=\alpha+\beta x\_{i}+\epsilon\_{i},i=1,\dots,N$, show that the variance of the OLS estimate for $\beta$ is $\sigma^{2}/Ns\_{x}^{2}$, where $\operatorname{Var}(\epsilon)=\sigma^{2}$ and $\operatorname{Var}(x\_{i})=s\_{x}^{2}$

Akan ditunjukkan $\operatorname{Var}(b) = \sigma^{2}/Ns\_{x}^{2} = \operatorname{Var}(\epsilon)/N\operatorname{Var}(x\_{i})$

Dari hasil sebelumnya,

$$b\_2 = \frac{N\sum x\_i y\_i - \sum x\_i \sum y\_i}{N\sum x\_i^2 - (\sum x\_i)^2}$$

Diketahui
$$
\begin{align}
\bar{x} & = \frac{1}{N}\sum x \\
\bar{y} & = \frac{1}{N}\sum y
\end{align}
$$

Perhatikan bahwa
$$
\begin{align}

\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})(y\_i - \bar{y}) & = \sum x\_i y\_i - N\bar{x}\bar{y} \\

\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2 & = \sum x\_i^2 - N\bar{x}^2

\end{align}
$$

Jadi,
$$b\_2 = \frac{\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})(y\_i - \bar{y})}{\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2}$$

Substitisukan $y\_i = \alpha + \beta x\_i + \epsilon\_i$, sehingga diperoleh:

$$
\begin{align} \\
b\_2 &= \frac{\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})(\alpha + \beta x\_i + \epsilon\_i - \bar{y})}{\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2} \\
&= \frac{\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})(\beta x\_i + \epsilon\_i - \beta\bar{x} - \bar{\epsilon})}{\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2} \\
&= \frac{\beta\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2 + \sum (x\_i - \bar{x})(\epsilon\_i - \bar{\epsilon})}{\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2} \\
&= \beta + \frac{\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})(\epsilon\_i - \bar{\epsilon})}{\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2}
\end{align}
$$

Karena $\sum(x\_{i}-\bar{x})=\sum x\_{i}- N\bar{x}=\sum x\_{i}-\sum x\_{i}=0$ maka $\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})\bar{\epsilon} = \bar{\epsilon}\sum(x\_i - \bar{x}) = 0$, sehingga,

$$b\_2 = \beta + \frac{\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})\epsilon\_i}{\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2}$$

Jadi,
$$
\begin{align}
\operatorname{Var}(b\_2) &= \operatorname{Var}\left(\frac{\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})\epsilon\_i}{\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2}\right) \\
&= \frac{1}{\[\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2]^2}\operatorname{Var}\left(\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})\epsilon\_i\right)
\end{align}
$$

dengan
$$
\begin{align}
\operatorname{Var}\left(\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})\epsilon\_i\right) &= \sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2 \operatorname{Var}(\epsilon\_i) \\
&= \sigma^2 \sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2
\end{align}
$$

Sehingga
$$\operatorname{Var}(b\_2) = \frac{\sigma^2 \sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2}{\[\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2]^2} = \frac{\sigma^2}{\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2}$$

Karena $s\_x^2 = \operatorname{Var}(x\_i) = \frac{1}{N}\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2$, dapat diperoleh $\sum (x\_i - \bar{x})^2 = Ns\_x^2$.

Jadi, $$\operatorname{Var}(b\_2) = \frac{\sigma^2}{Ns\_x^2}$$

## Exercise 2.1 (regression) buku

> Consider the following linear regression model:
> $$ y\_{i}=\beta\_{1}+\beta\_{2}x\_{i2}+\beta\_{3}x\_{i3}+\epsilon\_{i}=x\_{i}'\beta+\epsilon\_{i} $$

### a

> a. Explain how the ordinary least squares estimator for $\beta$ is determined, and derive an expression for $b$

Estimator OLS untuk $\beta$, didapat dengan meminimalkan SSE:
$$
S(\beta) = \sum\_{i=1}^N (y\_{i}-x\_{i}'\beta)
$$

Dengan menurunkan $S(\beta)=0$, diperoleh
$$
\begin{align}
S'(\beta) = -2 \sum\_{i=1}^N x\_{i}(y\_{i}-x\_{i}'b)  & = 0 \\
\sum\_{i=1}^N (x\_{i}y\_{i} - x\_{i}x\_{i}'b) & = 0 \\
\sum\_{i=1}^N x\_{i}y\_{i} & = \sum\_{i=1}^Nx\_{i}x\_{i}'b
\end{align}
$$

Asumsikan bahwa $\sum\_{i=1}^N x\_{i}x'_{i}$ adalah matriks invertibel. Maka dapat diperoleh OLS estimator untuk $\beta$, yang dinotasikan dengan $b$
$$
b = \left( \sum_{i=1}^N x\_{i}'x\_{i} \right)^{-1}\sum\_{i=1}^Nx\_{i}y\_{i}
$$

### b.

> Which assumptions to make $b$ an unbiased estimator for $\beta$?

1. $E\[\epsilon\_{i}]=0,\quad\forall i$
2. $E\[\epsilon\_{i}|x\_{i}]=0,\quad\forall i$

### c.

> Explain how a confidence interval for $\beta\_{2}$ can be constructed. Which additional assumptions are needed?

Dengan asumsi A1-5,
$$
\begin{align}
b\_{i}  & \sim N(\beta\_{i},\sigma^{2}(X'X)^{-1}) \\
\frac{b\_{i}-\beta\_{i}}{\sqrt{ \sigma^{2}(X'X)^{-1}}} & \sim N(0,1)
\end{align}
$$

Diketahui $E\[s^{2}]=\sigma^{2}$ dengan $s^{2}=\frac{1}{N-k}\sum\hat{\epsilon}_{i}^{2}$. Maka,
$$
SE(\beta_{i})=\frac{b\_{i}-\beta\_{i}}{\sqrt{ \sigma^{2}(X'X)^{-1}}} \sim t\_{N-k}
$$

Jadi,
$$ b\_{i} \pm t\_{\alpha/2;N-k}\cdot SE(b\_{i}) $$

### d.

> Explain how one can test the hypothesis that $\beta\_{3}=1$

Hipotesis:

- $H\_{0}$ : $\beta\_{3}=1$
- $H\_{1}$ : $\beta\_{3}\neq1$

Statistik uji:
$$
\begin{align}
t & = \frac{b\_{i}-\beta\_{3}}{SE(b\_{i})}\sim t\_{N-k} \\
& = \frac{b\_{3}-1}{SE(b\_{3})}\sim t\_{N-3}
\end{align}
$$

Perhatikan bahwa dibawah A1-A4, $t\_{N-k}\xrightarrow{D}z$

Tolak $H\_{0}$, apabila $|t|>z\_{\alpha/2}$
