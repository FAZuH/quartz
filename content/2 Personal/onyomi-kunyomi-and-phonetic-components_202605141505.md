## Kanji Phonetic Components (Usagi deck)

- \~60% of jouyou kanji have components that hint at their on'yomi
- Learning ~100 such components lets you infer readings of new kanji
- Only applies to on'yomi, not kun'yomi

## On'yomi vs Kun'yomi

- **On'yomi**: Chinese-derived reading, used in kanji compounds (電話、学校)
- **Kun'yomi**: native Japanese reading, used standalone or with okurigana (食べる、山)

## Heuristic

- Kanji + okurigana → likely kun'yomi
- Kanji compound, no okurigana → likely on'yomi
- Not a law, ~80% reliable

## Exceptions

- 頑張る: 張 reads ば(る) — kun'yomi despite being in a compound
- 仕事: mixed reading — called 湯桶読み (yutōyomi)

## Mixed Reading Types

- 湯桶読み (yutōyomi): first kanji on'yomi, second kun'yomi — e.g. 仕事 (し+ごと)
- 重箱読み (jūbakoyomi): first kanji kun'yomi, second on'yomi — e.g. 重箱 itself (じゅう+ばこ)

## Why phonetic components don't help with kun'yomi

- The phonetic inheritance is Chinese, so it only carries over to on'yomi readings. Kun'yomi are native Japanese and don't follow that pattern.
