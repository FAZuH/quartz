## Rumus

- Norm: $||\mathbf{v}||= \sqrt{ v\_{1}^{2}+v\_{2}^{2}+\dots+v\_{r}^{2} }$
- Inner prod:
  - $\langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{v} \rangle=u\_{1}v\_{1}+u\_{2}v\_{2}+\dots+u\_{r}v\_{r}$
  - $A\cdot B=\operatorname{tr}(A^T B)$
- Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization:
  - $\mathbf{v}_{i}=\mathbf{u}_{i}-\sum\_{j=1}^{i-1} \frac{\langle \mathbf{u}_{i},\mathbf{v}_{j} \rangle}{||\mathbf{v}_{j}||^{2}}\mathbf{v}_{j}$
  - $B\_{i}=A\_{i}-\sum\_{j=1}^{i-1} \frac{A\_{i}\cdot B\_{j}}{B\_{j}\cdot B\_{j}}B\_{j}$
- Orthonormal/normalizing:
  - $\mathbf{u}=\frac{1}{||\mathbf{v}||}\mathbf{v}$
  - $B=||A||^{-1}A$
- Orthogonal: $\mathbf{u}\cdot \mathbf{v}=0$
- QR Decomposition
  - $A=QR$ where $Q$ has orthonormal cols, $R$ upper triangular
  - $Q=\[\mathbf{q}_{1};\cdots;\mathbf{q}_{n}]$ from normalizing Gram-Schmidt
  - $R : r\_{ij}=\langle \mathbf{a}_{j},\mathbf{q}_{i} \rangle,\quad i\leq j$
- Basis
  - Col space: Cols of $A$ corresponding to pivot positions of $A$ (from RREF)
  - Row space: Cols of $A^T$ corresponding to pivot positions of $A^T$
- Dimension: Count of basis of vector space
- Rank
  - Dimension of col/row space (equal)
  - Count of pivot cols
  - $\operatorname{rank}(A)=\operatorname{rank}(T)+\operatorname{rank}(W-VT^{-1}U)$
- G-Inverse
  1. $r\times r$ invertible submatrix $M$, $r=\operatorname{rank}(A)$
  2. Create $n\times m$ [[def-zero-matrix_202509241049|zero matrix]], and insert $(M^{-1})^T$ where $M$ was taken from $A$
  3. Matrix from (2) is $(A^{-1})^T$
- Partition
  - Product: Treat partitioned as elements of $A$, and multiply normally, then expand
  - Inverse:
    1. $t=T^{-1}$, $u=tU$, $v=Vt$, $q=(W-vU)^{-1}$
    2. $$\begin{bmatrix} T & U \ V & W \end{bmatrix}^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix} t & 0 \ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} -u \ I\_{n} \end{bmatrix}q\begin{bmatrix} -v & I\_{n} \end{bmatrix}$$ atau $$ \begin{bmatrix} W & V \ U & T \end{bmatrix}^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 \ 0 & t \end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix} I\_{n} \ -u \end{bmatrix}q\begin{bmatrix} I\_{n} & -v \end{bmatrix} $$
