## Rumus

- Norm: $||\mathbf{v}||= \sqrt{ v_{1}^{2}+v_{2}^{2}+\dots+v_{r}^{2} }$
- Inner prod:
	- $\langle \mathbf{u},\mathbf{v} \rangle=u_{1}v_{1}+u_{2}v_{2}+\dots+u_{r}v_{r}$
	- $A\cdot B=\operatorname{tr}(A^T B)$
- Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization:
	- $\mathbf{v}_{i}=\mathbf{u}_{i}-\sum_{j=1}^{i-1} \frac{\langle \mathbf{u}_{i},\mathbf{v}_{j} \rangle}{||\mathbf{v}_{j}||^{2}}\mathbf{v}_{j}$
	- $B_{i}=A_{i}-\sum_{j=1}^{i-1} \frac{A_{i}\cdot B_{j}}{B_{j}\cdot B_{j}}B_{j}$
- Orthonormal/normalizing:
	- $\mathbf{u}=\frac{1}{||\mathbf{v}||}\mathbf{v}$
	- $B=||A||^{-1}A$
- Orthogonal: $\mathbf{u}\cdot \mathbf{v}=0$
- QR Decomposition
	- $A=QR$ where $Q$ has orthonormal cols, $R$ upper triangular 
	- $Q=[\mathbf{q}_{1}\;\cdots\;\mathbf{q}_{n}]$ from normalizing Gram-Schmidt 
	- $R : r_{ij}=\langle \mathbf{a}_{j},\mathbf{q}_{i} \rangle,\quad i\leq j$
- Basis 
	- Col space: Cols of $A$ corresponding to pivot positions of $A$ (from RREF)
	- Row space: Cols of $A^T$ corresponding to pivot positions of $A^T$ 
- Dimension: Count of basis of vector space
- Rank
	- Dimension of col/row space (equal)
	- Count of pivot cols
	- $\operatorname{rank}(A)=\operatorname{rank}(T)+\operatorname{rank}(W-VT^{-1}U)$
- G-Inverse
	1. $r\times r$ invertible submatrix $M$, $r=\operatorname{rank}(A)$
	2. Create $n\times m$ [[3 Reference/def-zero-matrix_202509241049\|zero matrix]], and insert $(M^{-1})^T$ where $M$ was taken from $A$
	3. Matrix from (2) is $(A^{-1})^T$
- Partition
	- Product: Treat partitioned as elements of $A$, and multiply normally, then expand
	- Inverse:
		1. $t=T^{-1}$, $u=tU$, $v=Vt$, $q=(W-vU)^{-1}$
		2. $$\begin{bmatrix} T & U \\ V & W \end{bmatrix}^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix} t & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} -u \\ I_{n} \end{bmatrix}q\begin{bmatrix} -v & I_{n} \end{bmatrix}$$ atau $$ \begin{bmatrix} W & V \\ U & T \end{bmatrix}^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & t \end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix} I_{n} \\ -u \end{bmatrix}q\begin{bmatrix} I_{n} & -v \end{bmatrix} $$
