# UAS statmat 2

## Distributions

Memorize pdf, mean, variance of:

- [[Continuous Distributions#Gamma distribution|Gamma distribution]] : pdf, $\alpha \beta$, $\alpha \beta^2$
- [[Continuous Distributions#Chi-square distribution|Chi-square distribution]] : $\Gamma(r/2, 2)$, $r$, $2r$
- [[Continuous Distributions#Normal distribution|Normal distribution]] : pdf
- [[Discrete Distributions#Bernoulli distribution|Bernoulli distribution]] : $p^x(1-p)^{1-x}$, $p$, $p(1-p)$
- [[Discrete Distributions#Binomial distribution|Binomial distribution]] : $\binom nx p^x(1-p)^{n-x}$, $np$, $np(1-p)$
- [[Discrete Distributions#Poisson distribution|Poisson distribution]] : pdf, $\lambda$, $\lambda$
  $$
  \begin{align}
  & \frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha)\beta^\alpha}x^{\alpha-1}e^{-x/\beta} \\
  & \frac{1}{\sigma \sqrt{ 2\pi }}\exp\left\[ -\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2} \right] \\
  & \frac{e^{-\lambda}\lambda^x}{x!}
  \end{align}
  $$

## Distribution relationships

$$
\begin{align}
\bar{X} & = \frac{1}{n} \sum X\_{i} \sim N\left( \mu, \frac{\sigma^2}{n} \right) \\
S^2 & = \frac{1}{n-1} \sum (X\_{i} - \bar{X})^2
\end{align}
$$

| **Random variables**                                   | **Transformation**                                       | **Distribution of transformation** |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$                                | $\dfrac{nS^2}{\sigma}$                                   | $\chi^2(n)$                        |
| $Z\sim N(0,1)$                                         | $\sum^kZ\_{i}$                                            | $\chi^2(k)$                        |
| $U\sim N(0,1)$<br>$V\sim \chi^2(r)$                    | $\dfrac{U}{\sqrt{ V / r }}$                              | $t(r)$                             |
| $U\sim \chi^2(r\_{1})$<br>$V\sim \chi^2(r\_{2})$         | $\dfrac{U / r\_{1}}{V / r\_{2}}$                           | $F(r\_{1},r\_{2})$                   |
| $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$                                | $\dfrac{\bar{X}-\mu}{S / \sqrt{ n }}$                    | $t(n-1)$                           |
| $N(\mu\_{1},\sigma^2\_{1})$<br>$N(\mu\_{2},\sigma^2\_{2})$ | $\dfrac{S^2\_{1} / \sigma^2\_{1}}{S^2\_{2} / \sigma^2\_{2}}$ | $F(n\_{1}-1,n\_{2}-1)$               |

## Definitions & theorems

### [[def-sufficient-statistic_202507170925|Sufficient Statistic]]

$$ \frac{\prod\_{i=1}^n f(x\_{i},\theta)}{f\_{Y\_{1}}\[u\_{1}(\mathbf{x});\theta]} \quad \text{independent of }\theta$$

[[theorem-neyman-theorem_202508052229|Neyman Theorem]]:

- $Y\_{1}=u\_{1}(\mathbf{x})$ [[Def-statistic|Statistic]] for $\theta$
- $$\exists k\_{1},k\_{2}\ni \prod f(x\_{i};\theta) = k\_{1}\[u\_{1}(\mathbf{x});\theta] \cdot k\_{2}(\mathbf{x})$$
- $k\_{2}(\mathbf{x})$ independent of $\theta$

### [[def-regular-exponential-class_202507170942|Regular Exponential Class]]

$$
f(x;\theta) = \begin{cases}
\exp\[p(\theta)K(x) + H(x) + q(\theta)] & x\in \mathcal{S} \\
0 & \text{elsewhere}
\end{cases}
$$

- $\mathcal{S}$ independent of $\theta$
- $p(\theta)$ nontrivial
- $X$ continuous:
  - $K'(x)\neq 0$
  - $H(x)$ continuous
- $X$ discrete:
  - $K(x)$ nontrivial

## [[def-fisher-information_202507170919|Fisher Information]]

$$
\begin{align}
I(\theta) & = E\left\[ \left( \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} \ln f(X;\theta) \right)^2 \right] \\
& = -E\left\[ \frac{\partial^2}{\partial \theta^2}\ln f(X;\theta) \right] \\
I\_{n}(\theta) & = nI(\theta)
\end{align}
$$

## [[def-rao-cramer-lower-bound_202507171109|Rao-Cramer]] & [[def-efficient-estimator_202507170919|Efficient Estimator]]

$$ \operatorname{Var}(Y) \geq \frac{\[k'(\theta)]^2}{I\_{n}(\theta)} $$

1. $Y$ is [[Def-unbiased-estimator|Unbiased Estimator]] for $\theta$, i.e., $E(Y) = \theta$
2. $$ \operatorname{Var}(Y) = \frac{1}{nI(\theta)}$$

## Confidence interval

**Pivot variable**: Function of estimator of $\theta$, where dist and param of dist of the pivot variable is known

1. <u>Find estimator</u> for $\theta$, e.g., $\hat{\theta}=\bar{X}$ or $\hat{\theta}=S^2$
2. <u>Find pivot variable</u> $Z$, i.e., function of $\hat{\theta}$, where dist and param of dist of $Z$ is known
3. <u>Compute confidence interval</u> $$ 1-\alpha = P\_{\mu}\[\Phi\<Z(\hat{\theta})<-\Phi] $$
4. Find <u>realization of confidence interval</u>

> [!warning]
> [[#Best test]] & [[#UMPT]] incomplete

## Best test

1. Determine hypotheses: $H\_{0} : \theta=\theta'$ vs $H\_{1}: \theta=\theta''$
2. Construct $\lambda=\dfrac{L(\theta';\mathbf{x})}{L(\theta'';\mathbf{x})}$
3. Apply [[theorem-neyman-pearson-theorem_202508052132|Neyman-Pearson Theorem]]. Best test has the form:
   - Reject $H\_{0}$ if $\lambda \leq k$
   - Accept $H\_{0}$ if $\lambda > k$
     Where $k>0$
4. Simplify test statistic:
   1. Write $\lambda\leq k$ in terms of $C={ \mathbf{x}:u(\mathbf{x};\theta',\theta'') \leq \text{(or }\geq)\ c }$. $c$ usually constant obtained from $k$
   2. Use $\ln \lambda= \ln \dfrac{ L(\theta';\mathbf{x})}{ L(\theta'';\mathbf{x})}$
      or both
5. Get $c$ (critical value) such that $\alpha=P\_{\theta'}(\mathbf{x}\in C)$

## UMPT

1. Determine hypotheses: $H\_{0} : \theta=\theta'$ vs $H\_{1}: \theta \in \Theta\_{1}$
2. Construct $\lambda\_{\theta\_{1}}=\dfrac{L(\theta';\mathbf{x})}{L(\theta\_{1};\mathbf{x})},\forall\theta\_{1} \in \Theta\_{1}$
3. Apply [[theorem-neyman-pearson-theorem_202508052132|Neyman-Pearson Theorem]] for each $\theta\_{1}$. Show critical region is the same:
   - Reject $H\_{0}$ if $\lambda\_{\theta\_{1}} \leq k\_{\theta\_{1}}$
   - Accept $H\_{0}$ if $\lambda\_{\theta\_{1}} > k\_{\theta\_{1}}$
     Where $k\_{\theta\_{1}}>0$
4. Simplify test statistic:
   1. Write $\lambda\_{\theta\_{1}}\leq k\_{\theta\_{1}}$ in terms of $C={ \mathbf{x}:u(\mathbf{x}) \leq \text{(or }\geq)\ c }$. Show $C$ is independent of $\theta\_{1}$
   2. Use $\ln \lambda\_{\theta\_{1}}= \ln \dfrac{ L(\theta';\mathbf{x})}{ L(\theta\_{1};\mathbf{x})}$
      or both
5. Get $c$ (critical value) such that $\alpha=P\_{\theta'}(\mathbf{x}\in C)$
