- Focus on recall speed. Just focus on how fast and easily you can recall the card.
- Don't obsess over the descriptions. The goal is to memorize the Kanji, not other bullshit.
- SPEEEEEEEEEEEEEEED

See [[choosing-recall-difficulty-of-anki-cards_202605031807|Choosing Recall Difficulty of Anki Cards]]

## The things to evaluate:

On Kanji -> Meaning:

1. Spelling
2. English meaning

On Meaning -> Kanji:

1. Spelling
2. Writing

> [!tip]
> Do this in order to make it easier

Choose the hardest difficulty between those two. e.g., on Meaning -> Kanji, if spelling is "good" but writing is "again", choose "again".
