$$
\begin{align}
SSE &= \sum(y\_i-\hat y\_i)^2 \\
SSR &= \sum(\hat y\_i - \bar y) ^2 \\
SST &= \sum(y\_i - \bar y)^2 = SSE + SSR \\
MSE &= SSE/\text{df} \\
RMSE & = \sqrt{ MSE }
\end{align}
$$

## Assumptions

Assumtions

1. $E\[\epsilon\_{i}]=0,\quad\forall i$
2. Exogeneous : $E\[\epsilon\_{i}|x\_{i}]=0$ (implied by A7)
3. Homoscedasticity : $\operatorname{Var}(\epsilon\_{i})=\sigma^{2},\quad\forall i$
4. No autocorrelation: $\operatorname{Cov}(\epsilon\_{i},\epsilon\_{j})=0,\quad\forall i\neq j$
5. $\epsilon\_{i}\sim N(0,\sigma^{2}),\quad\forall i$
6. $\frac{1}{N}(X'X)$ converges
7. $E\[x\_{i}\epsilon\_{i}]=0,\quad\forall i$ (implied by A8)
8. $x\_{i}$ and $\epsilon\_{i}$ independent $\forall i$
9. $\operatorname{Var}(\epsilon|X)=\sigma\Psi$
10. $E\[\epsilon|X]=0$
11. $\epsilon\_{t}\sim IID(0,\sigma^{2})$ (implies A1,3,4)
12. $\epsilon\_{t}$ serially uncorrelated, $E\[\epsilon\_{t}]=0$

Consequence

- $E\[b]=\beta$ (A1-2)
- A1-4
  - $\operatorname{Var}(b|X)=\sigma^{2}(X'X)^{-1}$
  - $E\[s^{2}]=\sigma^{2}$
  - $b$ BLUE (Best Linear Unbiased Estimator)
- $b\sim(\beta,\sigma^{2}(X'X)^{-1})$ (A1-5)
- $\epsilon\_i\sim NID(0,\sigma^{2}),\quad\forall i$ (A,3-5)
- $b\_{k}\xrightarrow{P}\beta\_{k}$ (A2,6; A6,7)
- $s^{2}\xrightarrow{P}\sigma^{2}$ (A3,6,7)

## OLS

- $b=(X'X)^{-1}X'Y=\operatorname{argmin}(SSE)$
- $\hat{y}=Xb$
- $\epsilon=y-\hat{y}$
- $SE(b\_{i})=\sqrt{ s^{2}(X'X)^{-1} }$
- $R^{2}=\frac{SSR}{SST}$
- $R\_{a}^{2}=1-\left[ \frac{N-1}{N-(k+1)} \right](1-R^{2})$

## Heteroskedastisitas

$\sigma\_{i}\neq\sigma\_{j}$ untuk suatu $i\neq j$

- Sebab: Data cross sectional, $\epsilon$ correlated with predictor
- Akibat:
  1. $\operatorname{Var}(b)$ tdk efisien => tdk BLUE (tetap tk bias & konsisten)
  2. $s^{2}$ bias
  3. $SE(b)$ bias & underestimate
  4. Inferensi misleading
- Solusi: Varians: HCCME, SE: White, model: WLS spy V(b) approx BLUE/GLS, transform log
- Uji: Graph, Breusch-Pagan, Koenker, Goldfeld-Quandt, White

## Autocorrelation

$Cov(\epsilon\_{i},\epsilon\_{j})\neq0$ (A4)

- Sebab: Omitted reg, incorrect func form/dynamic
- Akibat: sama dg hetero, $r$ bias tapi konsisten
- Uji: Correlogram residual, Durbin Watson, Box Pierce, Preusch-Godfrey, Ljung-Box
- Solusi:
  - Varians & SE: HAC-estimator for $\operatorname{Var}((B))$/Newey-West estimator
  - Transf model: Cochiane-Orcutt-estimator/FGLS estimator (quasi-diff)
  - Func form: $y\to \log(y)$
  - Add regressor: lags, seasonal dummies

## Multicollinearity

Correlated predictors

- Akibat: tdk efisien (varian inflated), difficult to identify individual impact
- Deteksi
  - Predictor high pairwise correlations
  - high $R^{2}$ but few significant coeffs
  - VIF > 10
- Solusi
  - Remove/combine corr variables
  - Use regularization (ridge reg, LASSO)
  - Collect more samples
  - principal component regression

## Endogen

$E\[x\_{i}\epsilon\_{i}]\neq 0$ untuk suatu $i$ (A7)

- Sebab:
  - Hub kausal 2 arah X\&Y (simultanitas)
  - Omitted var bias -> Bias
  - Measurment error -> $\beta\_{i}$ bias, inkonsisten, underestimate
  - Dynamic regression (eg $Y\_{t-1}$) -> Inkonsisten
  - Unobserved heterogeneity
- Akibat: Estimasi bias & inkonsisten
- Solusi: Instrumental variables (IV)
- Uji (nama & $H\_{0}$):
  - Wu Hausman: $\not\exists$endogen
  - Sargan: Semua instrumen valid
  - Cragg-Donald: Instrumen lemah
- GMM Estimation
  - Memperbaiki TSLS (momennya?)
  - Mencari bobot optimal
  - Hasil: Konsisten, efisien, asimptotik normal
